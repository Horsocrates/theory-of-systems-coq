*“Absolute truth above all”*

## What We Carry

We carry wisdom: we help others see absolute truth and understand it—in accordance with the [[Code of Ethics]] ([[Charter]], Article 1).

## What We Stand On

The building of knowledge stands on one foundation—[[Logic]]: from the first distinction to [[Metaphysics|metaphysics]], [[Theory of Systems|the theory of systems]], [[Epistemology|epistemology]], [[Ethics|ethics]], [[Social Systems|social systems]], [[Architecture of Reasoning|the architecture of reasoning]], and [[Mathematics|mathematics]]. Everything is derived, not postulated; the derivation is open to view in the [[Library]], and where it has been brought to a formal record, a machine checks its model ([[Formalization]]).

## Who Walks the Path

The path is open to everyone. The levels are listener (not a member) · follower · philosopher ([[Charter]], Article 2); the task of the follower is **to find truth rightly**, and that of the philosopher **to carry wisdom** ([[Code of Ethics]]).

## The Community

The two names are two sides of one community: “Faith in Wisdom” is who we are, and “the Path of Wisdom” is how we walk ([[Charter]]). The community lives by the [[Charter]]: founding, membership, structure, governance, community life, analysis of failures, funds, the community in the world.

## Joining

The path begins with a promise to oneself to follow Truth—entry is immediate; the condition of membership in the community is a quarterly donation ([[Charter]], Article 2.2). To get in touch, see [[Contacts]].
