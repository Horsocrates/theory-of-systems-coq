# The Single Ground of Knowledge

The building of knowledge stands on one foundation—[[Logic]]: from the first distinction to [[Metaphysics|metaphysics]], [[Theory of Systems|the theory of systems]], [[Epistemology|epistemology]], [[Ethics|ethics]], [[Social Systems|social systems]], [[Architecture of Reasoning|the architecture of reasoning]], and [[Mathematics|mathematics]].

<div class="directions">
  <a class="internal card" href="./library/branches/logic/index"><span class="t">Logic</span><span class="d">The foundation of the building of knowledge: the five laws of logic—properties of being itself, given together with it.</span></a>
  <a class="internal card" href="./library/branches/metaphysics"><span class="t">Metaphysics</span><span class="d">Ontological principles: being and the witness, the manifestations of Logic.</span></a>
