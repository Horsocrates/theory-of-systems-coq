Here is the registry of what is not and cannot be: impossible constructions and the substitutions by which they are built.

The modal square of the objective field holds three layers—“cannot not be,” “can be—and is,” “can be, but is not” ([[Metaphysics#The Objective and the Subjective|“Metaphysics”]]). The fourth corner—**“is not, and cannot be”**—does not enter the field: the impossible does not exist. Yet it is spoken of, because it is **built**: an impossible construction is an error of construction, and all the entries of the registry share one mechanism—**category substitution**: the contents of one category are passed off as another. A process is passed off as an object, a role as a thing, an element as a role, a status as a system, a position as a build, an absence as an object. A substitution is never legal: only a construction that keeps its category is legal.

The form of an entry: **the construction**—which substitution builds it—**the exact name** of what really stands behind the word. The registry is structured: root substitutions have derivatives. The list is open; a new entry must present its substitution—this is the working rule of the registry.

## Nothing

**“Nothing” as an object or a place**—reified absence: negation taken as a thing. There is no object “nothing”—there is the absence of an object; there is no place “nowhere”—there is the absence of a place.

- **The view from nowhere**—a derivative: a position in a place that does not exist—a view from nothing.[^1] In reality: objectivity is not a place outside all places but the witness’s orientation toward what does not depend on its position ([[Objectivity and Subjectivity#The Witness Is Subjective: Objectivity Is an Orientation|“Objectivity and Subjectivity”]]).

## Records Passed Off as an Agent

**The ego as an agency** (“the ego wants,” “the ego defends itself”)—an element passed off as a role: activity is ascribed to the zone of records about oneself. Records do not want—the witness wants ([[Metaphysics#The Ego Zone|the Ego zone]]).

- **The death of the ego**—a derivative: to die, the ego would have to be a thing, an organism; the zone of records is not an organism, and the witness of the report is alive—the “I” in the report points as it always pointed. In reality: disidentification and the potentialization of the zone; the records return—the return is guaranteed: the will to be is never zero.

## A Process Taken as an Object

- **A completed corpus, omniscience as an object**—the process of knowing declared a finished object of possession. In reality: all-embracingness in the course; there is no ready-made corpus ([[Epistemology#The Gap and Incompletability|“Epistemology”]]).
- **Actual infinity**—a process without a last step declared a ready “everything.” In reality: process infinity is a process open to continuation ([[Mathematics#Processes and Infinity|“Mathematics”]]).
	- **An occupied role-limit**—a derivative: the process is declared to have arrived—the role “where it is all going” is ascribed an element that no step of the rules gives. In reality: approach without arrival; “it will not be occupied” is not a prohibition but a conclusion of the observer, held by the completeness of the rules: a theorem about the system ([[Theory of Systems#The Boundary of the Theory: The Role-Limit|the boundary of the theory]]).

## Standalone Entries

- **Something unknowable in principle**—non-existence passed off as a mystery: to think the unknowable is to distinguish it, that is, to recognize it as determinate, and the determinate is knowable. In reality: there is only the not-yet-known ([[Epistemology#Knowability|“Knowability”]]).
- **“Knew by chance”**—a coincidence passed off as knowledge: a coincidence has no ground connecting the representation with how it is; so too in Gettier cases, where the opinion is justified but what makes it true is a coincidence. In reality: true opinion; one can coincide with absolute truth by chance; one can come to it only by a path ([[Truth and Absolute Truth#The Rungs of Representation|“Truth and Absolute Truth”]]).
- **Will = 0 in what exists**—a change of status passed off as destruction: “no will to be” would read as non-being. In reality: the minimum of the scale is one, the unmanifest, “can be, but is not”; manifestation returns—the return is guaranteed ([[Metaphysics#The Operator and the Will|the scale of the will]]).
- **A complete self-model from within**—the inner position passed off as an outside view of oneself: in knowing itself, the witness is inside the system it knows. In reality: an objective view of oneself is a labor of its own: one needs another witness or a rise by a tier ([[Objectivity and Subjectivity#The Witness Is Subjective: Objectivity Is an Orientation|“Objectivity and Subjectivity”]]).
- **Position as build**—naive objectivism: how a system is given from a position is passed off as its build. In reality: the objective is attained by a path, from one’s own position ([[Objectivity and Subjectivity#Two Edges: Naive Objectivism and Relativism|the two edges]]).
- **The irrational as a property of reality**—the status of a wrong construction passed off as a property of the world. In reality: everything that exists is organized by logic and is therefore rational; only what is wrongly built is irrational (analysis below—[[#The Rational and the Irrational|“The Rational and the Irrational”]]).
- **A paradox in reality**—a defect of representation passed off as a property of the object: a paradox points to the place of the defect—in construction, in premises, or in expectation; a “paradoxical object” was never an object. In reality: in a well-formed statement a paradox is impossible ([[Paradox Catalog|“Why There Is No Paradox”]]).
- **A change of essence as a change of *A***—another *A* passed off as the same *A* changed: a change in the status of a necessary role is not a change of the object but another criterion, another system. In reality: manifestation and composition change freely under the same constitution—*A* = *A* stands ([[Identity]]).
- **The return of a past state**—a slice passed off as a state: the full state is attached to the step, and the step is time; only a slice can return—a projection, for example the arrangement of the pieces, not a state. In reality: the repetition of a slice at a new step—three occurrences of the same board are three different states, and the rules themselves assign them different consequences ([[Theory of Systems#A System in Time: Dynamics|dynamics]]).

## The Rational and the Irrational

The word “rational” comes from Latin, and the arc of its senses forms a single series: *ratio* ← *reor* “to reckon”—reckoning, calculation, account; relation; order and ground; reason. The Greek λόγος (logos) spanned the same arc: reckoning · relation · word · law; Latin *ratio* coincides with it in the senses “reckoning,” “ground,” “reason,” and “law,” and served as its translation, while λόγος in the sense of “word” was rendered in Latin as *sermo* and *verbum*. “Irrational” is one negation over the whole arc at once: without relation, without reckoning, without ground, without reason. Which link is negated primarily is suggested by the school name of [[Sufficient Reason|the Law of Sufficient Reason]]—*principium rationis sufficientis*: *ratio* is the ground itself; “nothing exists without a ground” (*nihil est sine ratione*, Leibniz). What has a ground is rational.

Hence the reading of the whole position. Everything that exists exists determinately—knowably and coherently, exists within logic and is organized by [[Logic|logic]]; and logic is always rational. Therefore **everything that exists is rational**. Irrationality is not found in reality as a property of it: it is a status of *construction*, and precisely of a wrong one—a construction denied being, the corner “is not, and cannot be.” The irrational is the generic name for impossible constructions: paradoxes, contradictory constructions, the impossible that an [[Impossible Goal|impossible goal]] outlines—a completed corpus of an unbounded process, a contradictory category. In reality there is nothing irrational—just as there are no paradoxes in it.

What, then, does the label “irrational” do in everyday speech? It breaks into three figures, and only the first is genuine:

- **The wrongly built**—the only genuine irrationality; it lives only in constructions, not in the world;
- **A process without completion**—“irrational numbers”: a role-limit is a fully rational process—there is a law, there is no completion by an element; the name was given by a distorted perception (case below);
- **A chain without visibility**—“irrational” fear and kindred labels: the causal link that produces the fear exists—and is therefore fully rational; the witness does not see it and merely assigns the object “to the group of the irrational.” The label describes the witness’s blindness but is ascribed to the object.

The second and third figures are one error: **a given of a position is passed off as absolute truth about the object**. “I cannot express the relation” and “I do not see the chain” are truth about the meeting; “there is no relation” and “there is no chain” are untruth about the object. This is the same substitution of status that is analyzed in the theme [[Truth and Absolute Truth#The Potential and Projection|“Truth and Absolute Truth”]].

> [!example]- Case: the unsayable number
> Late tradition ascribes to the Pythagoreans the discovery that the side and the diagonal of a square are incommensurable. The Arabic translators rendered Euclid’s ἄλογος (alogos) as *aṣamm*, “deaf” (the rational was *munṭaq*, “endowed with speech”); hence Latin *surdus* (Gerard of Cremona, twelfth century) and English *surd*: an irrational root is literally “deaf,” and in a secondary sense “mute.”
>
> What is exact here and what is distorted are told apart by the tiers of speech. To say the magnitude *as an element*—as a finite ratio of two integers—is impossible: no pair p/q pronounces the square root of 2; in this sense the number is indeed mute. But it can be said by a *rule*: “that which, squared, gives 2”; the law of the Pell process is short: it is stated as a whole and determines the process completely. The object is mute by the element—and speaks by the rule. The name was given at the tier of elements: the incommensurable is not expressed by a ratio of two numbers—hence the “unsayable” in Plato and ἄλογος, “having no ratio,” in Euclid; the ancients knew the rule too—the “side and diagonal numbers” of Theon of Smyrna give the same series 1/1, 3/2, 7/5, 17/12. Both halves—the muteness of the element and the speech of the rule—are proved ([[Formalization#What Is Not, and Cannot Be|“Formalization”]]).
>
> The way it was established is telling as well: incommensurability was proved by pure reasoning—by contradiction (Aristotle, *Prior Analytics* I.23, 41a26–27: the assumption of commensurability leads to odd numbers being equal to even ones); the “a-logos” was established by logos itself, and in this alone rationality by the rule can already be seen. The full frame is [[Mathematics#The Finitization Boundary|the finitization boundary]].

> [!example]- Case: fear without a cause
> Fear “without a cause” carries its chain: bodily memory, an unseen trigger, the trace of an earlier meeting. The chain is there and is rational; it is simply not seen. “Causeless” is a report about the witness’s slice passed off as a report about the object; the exact form of the label is “I do not yet see the cause.”

Kindred positions are well known: Parmenides (“to think and to be are one and the same”; on another reading of the fragment, “it is the same thing that can be thought and that can be”; our translation), the Stoic logos, Leibniz’s *nihil est sine ratione*, Hegel’s “what is rational is actual; and what is actual is rational.”[^2] What sets the reading here apart: the irrational is exclusively a status of wrong construction. It is this corner, “is not, and cannot be,” that the present registry gathers: each entry above shows the substitution by which it is built.

## Summary

1. **The impossible does not exist—it is built.** The fourth corner of the modal square, “is not, and cannot be,” does not enter the field: an impossible construction is an error of construction.
2. **All the entries of the registry share one mechanism—category substitution:** the contents of one category are passed off as another. A substitution is never legal.
3. **The registry is structured:** from reified nothing—the view from nowhere; from the ego as agent—the death of the ego; a process taken as an object—a completed corpus and actual infinity, and from the latter an occupied role-limit; alongside—standalone entries.
4. **Everything that exists is rational; only what is wrongly built is irrational.** The label “irrational” in everyday speech breaks into three figures—the wrongly built, a process without completion, a chain without visibility; the second and third are a given of a position passed off as absolute truth about the object.
5. **The list is open:** a new entry must present its substitution.
