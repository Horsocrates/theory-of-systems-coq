Eight entrances to one building—the [[Library]]; the theses of the entrances are set out there. The order of the entrances is the order of derivation:

- [[Logic]]
- [[Metaphysics]]
- [[Theory of Systems]]
- [[Epistemology]]
- [[Ethics]]
- [[Social Systems]] (in development)
- [[Architecture of Reasoning]]
- [[Mathematics]]
