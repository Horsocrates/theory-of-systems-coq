**5.1.1** · [[05 Context Methods|Context-Dependent Methods (Type 5)]]

## As a Method (Type 5)

**5.1.1 · [[5.1 Intuitive Cognition\|5.1 Intuitive Cognition]].** The method is valid under its conditions; the task is discernment, and only outside them would it be ruled out.

**When it is valid.** Genuine intuition is the integrated experience of an expert (a physician, an investor, a chess player)

**When it is a fallacy.** Taking emotion, prejudice, or wishful thinking for intuition

**Mechanism of discernment.** Genuine intuition: calm confidence, independent of desires. False intuition: emotionally charged, coinciding with desires.

**Valid:** An experienced physician “feels” that something is wrong with a patient—and turns out to be right

**Fallacious:** “I feel that this lottery ticket is a winner”—wishful thinking
