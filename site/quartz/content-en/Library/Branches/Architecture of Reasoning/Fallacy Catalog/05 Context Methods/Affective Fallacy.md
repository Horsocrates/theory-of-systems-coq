**5.1.2** · [[05 Context Methods|Context-Dependent Methods (Type 5)]]

## As a Method (Type 5)

**5.1.2 · [[5.1 Intuitive Cognition\|5.1 Intuitive Cognition]].** The method is valid under its conditions; the task is discernment, and only outside them would it be ruled out.

**When it is valid.** In aesthetics, ethics, and phenomenology, emotion is a valid source of knowledge

**When it is a fallacy.** Emotion replaces factual checking where facts are needed

**Mechanism of discernment.** Valid: “This is beautiful” (aesthetics), “This is disgusting” (moral intuition). Fallacy: “I am afraid, so it is dangerous.”

**Valid:** Revulsion at cruelty is a valid moral guide

**Fallacious:** “I don’t like this candidate” as the only criterion for voting
