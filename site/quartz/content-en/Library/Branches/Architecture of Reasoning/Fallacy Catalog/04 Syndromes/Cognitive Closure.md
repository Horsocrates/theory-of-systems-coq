**4.2.2** · [[04 Syndromes|Syndromes (Type 4)]] → [[4.2 Systemic Distortion|4.2 Systemic Distortion]] · subtype: Systemic Closure

**Formulation.** A need for certainty that leads to premature closing off from new information

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reflection (D6) is blocked systemically: any uncertainty causes discomfort, and closure brings relief.

**Example.** “It’s all clear to me—don’t confuse me with facts”; refusal to revisit decisions

**Diagnostic question.** Am I ready to change my opinion in light of new data?

**Psychological source.** Need for closure, intolerance of ambiguity

**Related fallacies:** 2.6.4, 2.6.2
