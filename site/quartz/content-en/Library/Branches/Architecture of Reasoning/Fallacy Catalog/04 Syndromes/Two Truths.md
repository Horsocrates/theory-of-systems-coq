**4.1.2** · [[04 Syndromes|Syndromes (Type 4)]] → [[4.1 Cognitive Dissonance|4.1 Cognitive Dissonance]] · subtype: Stable Dissonance

**Formulation.** One “truth” in one context and a contradictory one in another—at the same time

**Mechanism of the violation.** A violation of the Law of Non-Contradiction: A and not-A are true in different “compartments.” “I know, but I don’t believe.”

**Example.** On the exam one answers by the textbook, in life one acts the opposite way—and notices no discord

**Diagnostic question.** Can both “truths” be true at the same time?

**Psychological source.** Cognitive dissonance, motivated reasoning

**Related fallacies:** [[Disciplinary Blinders]], [[Gaslighting]]
