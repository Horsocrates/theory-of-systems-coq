**4.1.1** · [[04 Syndromes|Syndromes (Type 4)]] → [[4.1 Cognitive Dissonance|4.1 Cognitive Dissonance]] · subtype: Multiple Localization (D1, D3, D4)

**Formulation.** The tendency to notice, seek, select, and spread only information that confirms existing beliefs

**Mechanism of the violation.** A syndrome that manifests in several domains: D1 (filtering—we see what confirms), D3 (choosing the framework to fit the result), D4 (tendentious comparison). The common cause is the wish for a particular result.

**Example.** If you have been told “you will meet a tall dark stranger,” you will be on the lookout for such people and marvel at the accuracy

**Diagnostic question.** Do I look for refuting data as thoroughly as for confirming data?

**Psychological source.** Confirmation bias (a cognitive bias)

**Related fallacies:** 2.1.3, 2.3.6, [[A Priori Argument]]
