**4.2.3** · [[04 Syndromes|Syndromes (Type 4)]] → [[4.2 Systemic Distortion|4.2 Systemic Distortion]] · subtype: Collective Suppression

**Formulation.** Collective suppression of critical thinking for the sake of consensus and harmony

**Mechanism of the violation.** Social pressure blocks reflection (D6) and distorts recognition (D1): disagreement = betrayal.

**Example.** The Challenger disaster; a board that unanimously approved a doomed plan—the doubters kept silent

**Diagnostic question.** Is it safe to voice disagreement in this group?

**Psychological source.** Conformity, social pressure

**Related fallacies:** [[Echo Chamber]], 1.B.12
