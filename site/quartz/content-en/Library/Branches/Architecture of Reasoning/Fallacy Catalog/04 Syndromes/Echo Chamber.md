**4.2.1** · [[04 Syndromes|Syndromes (Type 4)]] → [[4.2 Systemic Distortion|4.2 Systemic Distortion]] · subtype: Self-Sustaining System

**Formulation.** A closed information environment in which only the existing opinion is confirmed

**Mechanism of the violation.** Data filtering (D1) + framework selection (D3) + absence of reflection (D6) create a stable system.

**Example.** A feed assembled solely from the like-minded; a club with no dissenters left

**Diagnostic question.** Do I hear alternative points of view? Who corrects me?

**Psychological source.** Filter bubble, groupthink

**Related fallacies:** [[Confirmation Bias]], 2.1.3, [[Deliberate Ignorance]]
