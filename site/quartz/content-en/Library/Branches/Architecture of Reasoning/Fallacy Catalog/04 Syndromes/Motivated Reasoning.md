**4.1.3** · [[04 Syndromes|Syndromes (Type 4)]] → [[4.1 Cognitive Dissonance|4.1 Cognitive Dissonance]] · subtype: Systemic Rationalization

**Formulation.** Systematic distortion of reasoning in favor of a desired conclusion

**Mechanism of the violation.** All domains are distorted in the direction of confirming the desired result. Rationalization instead of reasoning.

**Example.** An investor finds reasons to hold on to a losing stock; a fan finds a referees’ conspiracy in every match

**Diagnostic question.** Is the reasoning directed at the search for absolute truth or at confirming what is desired?

**Psychological source.** Wishful thinking, self-deception

**Related fallacies:** [[Confirmation Bias]], [[Two Truths|Two Truths / Compartmentalization]]
