**2.6.4.11** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.6 Domain 6 Reflection|2.6 Reflection]] · subtype: Immunization from Testing

**Formulation.** Ignoring information from experts a priori because “they all lie”

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reflection is blocked by hyper-cynicism: “you can’t fool me” = a refusal of all information.

**Example.** “Advertising works on everyone but me”—and so I watch it without any defenses

**Diagnostic question.** What is the belief that all sources lie based on?

**Psychological source.** Over-correction, reactance

**Related fallacies:** [[Deliberate Ignorance]], [[Simpletons Fallacy|Simpleton’s Fallacy]], [[Big Lie Technique]]
