**2.1.2.8** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.1 Domain 1 Recognition|2.1 Recognition]] · subtype: Object Substitution (A→B)

**Formulation.** Marginalizing opponents on the grounds of their alleged smell or “dirtiness”

**Mechanism of the violation.** Object substitution: instead of the arguments, the opponent’s imagined smell is evaluated. Dehumanization through disgust.

**Example.** “I’ll talk to you when you look decent”; “This proposal stinks”—instead of an analysis

**Diagnostic question.** Am I evaluating the arguments or the opponent’s physical characteristics?

**Psychological source.** Disgust response, prejudice

**Related fallacies:** [[Ad Hominem Argument]], [[Othering]]
