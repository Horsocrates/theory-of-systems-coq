**2.6.4.5** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.6 Domain 6 Reflection|2.6 Reflection]] · subtype: Immunization from Testing

**Formulation.** A response to criticism: “It’s a free country, I can say what I want!”

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reflection is dismissed: the right to speak is put in place of the groundedness of what is said. “I have the right to say this” ≠ “this is true.” Freedom of speech protects against censorship, not against criticism.

**Example.** “I can say what I want!”—in response to a request for justification

**Diagnostic question.** Am I defending my right to speak or the truth-value of what I say?

**Psychological source.** Confusing having the right with being right

**Related fallacies:** 2.6.4, 2.1.2
