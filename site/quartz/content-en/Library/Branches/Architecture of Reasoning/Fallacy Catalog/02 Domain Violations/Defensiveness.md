**2.6.3.2** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.6 Domain 6 Reflection|2.6 Reflection]] · subtype: Past Investment Influence

**Formulation.** After a decision is made—automatic defense of that decision and irrational rejection of alternatives

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reflection is distorted by a past choice: once a decision is made, it is defended regardless of new information. To admit an error = to devalue the investment.

**Example.** “Yes, I voted for Smith. He turned out to be a crook, but I still think that at the time he was better than the alternatives!”

**Diagnostic question.** Am I defending my decision because it is right or because it is mine?

**Psychological source.** Cognitive dissonance, sunk cost

**Related fallacies:** [[Argument from Inertia]], 4.1
