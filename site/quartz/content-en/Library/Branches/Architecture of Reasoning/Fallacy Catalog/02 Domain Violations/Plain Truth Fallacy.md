**2.2.3.2** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.2 Domain 2 Clarification|2.2 Clarification]] · subtype: Incomplete Analysis

**Formulation.** Preferring simple explanations to complex ones that are closer to absolute truth

**Mechanism of the violation.** Incomplete clarification: complexity is reduced to a “plain truth.” Monocausal explanations instead of multifactorial ones.

**Example.** “Just tell me the Plain Truth”; “I don’t need philosophy—just tell me why”

**Diagnostic question.** Is a simple explanation sufficient for this complexity?

**Psychological source.** Cognitive laziness, salience bias

**Related fallacies:** [[Overexplanation|Overexplanation / Mansplaining]], Snow Job
