**2.6.1.3** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.6 Domain 6 Reflection|2.6 Reflection]] · subtype: Illusion of Completion

**Formulation.** The claim that something “is what it is” in its essence and cannot change

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reflection is closed: the current state is declared an unchangeable “essence” or “nature.” Change is declared impossible a priori. An irrefutable claim.

**Example.** “He’s lazy by nature—he won’t change”; “being a latecomer is a diagnosis”

**Diagnostic question.** Is this an unchangeable “essence” or a current state that is capable of changing?

**Psychological source.** Cognitive simplicity, the fundamental attribution error

**Related fallacies:** [[Default Bias]], [[Appeal to Nature]]
