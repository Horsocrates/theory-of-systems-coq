**2.5.2.7** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.5 Domain 5 Inference|2.5 Inference]] · subtype: Causal Error

**Formulation.** Relying on “gut feeling” instead of reason or evidence

**Mechanism of the violation.** A false ground: “I feel” ≠ “it is so.” Those who trust their gut are more susceptible to fake news.

**Example.** “My heart tells me”; “I feel that it’s true”—without checking

**Diagnostic question.** Does the gut feeling have an evidential basis?

**Psychological source.** Intuition bias, cognitive ease

**Related fallacies:** [[Deliberate Ignorance]], [[Affective Fallacy Follow Your Heart|Affective Fallacy]]
