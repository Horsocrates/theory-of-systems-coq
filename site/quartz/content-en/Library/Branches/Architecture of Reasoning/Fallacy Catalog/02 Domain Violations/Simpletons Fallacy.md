**2.4.1.3** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.4 Domain 4 Comparison|2.4 Comparison]] · subtype: False Equation

**Formulation.** “My ignorance is as good as your knowledge”

**Mechanism of the violation.** False equation: ignorance is equated with knowledge. Democracy does not mean equality of competence.

**Example.** “Everyone’s opinion is equally valuable”—even the expert’s and the layman’s; the film *Forrest Gump*

**Diagnostic question.** Are the opinions equal in their grounding or only in their right to be voiced?

**Psychological source.** Anti-intellectualism, false equality

**Related fallacies:** [[Just Plain Folks]], [[Plain Truth Fallacy|Plain Truth Fallacy / KISS Principle]]
