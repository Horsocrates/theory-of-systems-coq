**2.6.1.4** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.6 Domain 6 Reflection|2.6 Reflection]] · subtype: Illusion of Completion

**Formulation.** Since we cannot foresee everything, any attempt to improve the world is doomed to failure

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reflection is closed by pessimism: “it won’t work anyway” as a universal conclusion. Uncertainty is raised to an absolute.

**Example.** “Every revolution devours its own children”—as an argument against any reform

**Diagnostic question.** Is uncertainty an argument against action?

**Psychological source.** Pessimism, learned helplessness

**Related fallacies:** TINA, [[Default Bias]]
