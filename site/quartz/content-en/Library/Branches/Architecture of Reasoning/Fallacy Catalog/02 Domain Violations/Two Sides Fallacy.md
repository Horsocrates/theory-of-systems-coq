**2.4.1.4** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.4 Domain 4 Comparison|2.4 Comparison]] · subtype: False Equation

**Formulation.** Presenting the sides as equally weighted when the consensus supports one of them

**Mechanism of the violation.** False equation: the scientific consensus and a marginal position are presented as equally valid.

**Example.** “The Earth is a sphere vs. flat and resting on a turtle. Both versions must be taught without preference”

**Diagnostic question.** Are the positions really equal in their grounding?

**Psychological source.** False equivalence, misguided fairness

**Related fallacies:** [[Simpletons Fallacy|Simpleton’s Fallacy]], 2.2.3
