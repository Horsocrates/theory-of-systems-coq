**2.5.2.6** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.5 Domain 5 Inference|2.5 Inference]] · subtype: Causal Error

**Formulation.** When something goes wrong, there is always someone else to put the blame on

**Mechanism of the violation.** False causality: the blame is assigned not to the real source but to a convenient target. Includes “blaming the victim.”

**Example.** “The new department is to blame for every failure!”; “It’s your own fault you got cheated”

**Diagnostic question.** Is the accused the real cause or a convenient target?

**Psychological source.** Deflection, othering

**Related fallacies:** 2.5.2, [[Affective Fallacy Follow Your Heart|Affective Fallacy]]
