**2.3.6.1** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.3 Domain 3 Framework Selection|2.3 Framework Selection]] · subtype: Framework for Result

**Formulation.** Proclaiming a general principle and immediately negating it with a “but” for a “special case”

**Mechanism of the violation.** A framework (a general principle) is chosen but then excluded ad hoc for a particular case without justification. “This is a special case” is not an argument but a refusal to apply the declared framework.

**Example.** “Everyone has the right to a trial by jury. BUT your crime is so horrible that it justifies life imprisonment without trial”

**Diagnostic question.** Why should this case be an exception? Is there a principled ground?

**Psychological source.** Self-justification, cognitive dissonance

**Related fallacies:** 2.4.3
