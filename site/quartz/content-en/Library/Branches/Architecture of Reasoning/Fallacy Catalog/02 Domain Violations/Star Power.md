**2.1.2.13** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.1 Domain 1 Recognition|2.1 Recognition]] · subtype: Object Substitution (A→B)

**Formulation.** Support for a position by a famous figure who is not an expert

**Mechanism of the violation.** Object substitution: instead of an evaluation of the argument, the authority of a celebrity. Includes quoting out of context.

**Example.** “An Olympic champion uses X—do you?”; a star advertises a diet they know nothing about

**Diagnostic question.** Is this celebrity an expert in this field?

**Psychological source.** Halo effect, celebrity worship

**Related fallacies:** Bandwagon, [[Ad Hominem Argument]]
