**2.1.2.7** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.1 Domain 1 Recognition|2.1 Recognition]] · subtype: Object Substitution (A→B)

**Formulation.** Any disagreement is declared racism/hatred; an argument is refuted by a mere label

**Mechanism of the violation.** Object substitution: instead of the argument (A), a label is stuck on the opponent (B). The label replaces analysis.

**Example.** “He’s a jerk, period”; “that’s a fallacy” without explaining why

**Diagnostic question.** Am I answering the argument or just sticking on a label?

**Psychological source.** Tribalism, cognitive shortcuts

**Related fallacies:** [[Ad Hominem Argument]], Reductionism
