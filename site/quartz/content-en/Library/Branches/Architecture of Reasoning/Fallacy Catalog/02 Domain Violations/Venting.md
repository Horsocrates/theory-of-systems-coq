**2.6.4.12** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.6 Domain 6 Reflection|2.6 Reflection]] · subtype: Immunization from Testing

**Formulation.** Words are exempt from criticism because the person was “just letting off steam”

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reflection is blocked: “I didn’t mean it”—although that is exactly what was said, and sincerely.

**Example.** “I didn’t say it seriously—I just snapped”; “everyone jokes like that”

**Diagnostic question.** Does “letting off steam” release one from responsibility?

**Psychological source.** Excuse-making, accountability avoidance

**Related fallacies:** [[Affective Fallacy Follow Your Heart|Affective Fallacy]], [[Political Correctness]]
