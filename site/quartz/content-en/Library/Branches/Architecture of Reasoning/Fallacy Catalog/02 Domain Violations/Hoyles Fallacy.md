**2.5.4.5** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.5 Domain 5 Inference|2.5 Inference]] · subtype: Scale Error / Exceeding the Grounds

**Formulation.** An event of low probability could not have happened / will never happen in reality

**Mechanism of the violation.** The conclusion exceeds its ground: “highly improbable” does not imply “impossible.” Low probability ≠ zero probability. Given enough time, improbable events happen.

**Example.** “The probability of such a deal is negligible—the deck is rigged!”—yet some deal always comes up

**Diagnostic question.** Does impossibility follow from low probability? How many attempts / how much time were there?

**Psychological source.** Misunderstanding of probability

**Related fallacies:** [[Argument from Incredulity]], [[Argument from Ignorance]]
