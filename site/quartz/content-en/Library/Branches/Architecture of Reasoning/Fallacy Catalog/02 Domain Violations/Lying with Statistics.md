**2.1.3.4** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.1 Domain 1 Recognition|2.1 Recognition]] · subtype: Data Filtering

**Formulation.** Misusing true figures to “prove” unrelated claims

**Mechanism of the violation.** Filtering and distortion: the data are true but are presented so as to create a false impression. Includes the “Tiny Percentage Fallacy.”

**Example.** “Education has never been cheaper—as a percentage of the national debt, it costs less than in 1965!”

**Diagnostic question.** Is the baseline of comparison relevant? Does the percentage hide the absolute magnitude?

**Psychological source.** The audience’s mathematical illiteracy

**Related fallacies:** [[Half Truth|Half Truth / Card Stacking]], 2.5.4
