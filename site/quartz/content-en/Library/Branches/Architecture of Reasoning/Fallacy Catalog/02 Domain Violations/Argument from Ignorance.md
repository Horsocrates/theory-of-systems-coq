**2.5.4.2** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.5 Domain 5 Inference|2.5 Inference]] · subtype: Scale Error / Exceeding the Grounds

**Formulation.** Since we cannot prove/disprove X, X is false/true

**Mechanism of the violation.** Absence of evidence is taken for evidence of absence (or the reverse). The conclusion exceeds its ground: from “we don’t know” follows neither “yes” nor “no.”

**Example.** “No one has proved that there is no monster in the lake. So there is one!”

**Diagnostic question.** Does any definite conclusion about X follow from “we don’t know X”?

**Psychological source.** Intolerance of uncertainty

**Related fallacies:** 2.2.3, [[A Priori Argument]]
