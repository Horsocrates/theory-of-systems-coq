**2.2.2.1** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.2 Domain 2 Clarification|2.2 Clarification]] · subtype: Hidden Agent

**Formulation.** Concealing the active agent behind the grammatical passive, creating an illusion of inevitability

**Mechanism of the violation.** Incomplete clarification: who acts is hidden. “It has been decided to dismiss you” instead of “I decided.” Responsibility is blurred.

**Example.** “Mistakes were made” instead of “we made mistakes”; “the vase got broken” instead of “I broke it”—the agent is hidden by the grammatical voice

**Diagnostic question.** Who exactly performed the action? Why is the agent hidden?

**Psychological source.** Avoidance of responsibility, bureaucratic language

**Related fallacies:** [[Political Correctness]], 2.1.2
