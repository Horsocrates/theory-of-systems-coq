**2.2.1.1** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.2 Domain 2 Clarification|2.2 Clarification]] · subtype: Meaning Drift

**Formulation.** An imposed punishment is described as a “natural consequence,” confusing causality and will

**Mechanism of the violation.** The term “consequence” drifts: in the premise it means a natural cause-and-effect connection, in the conclusion an arbitrarily imposed punishment. The equivocation lends the punishment a false ethos of inevitability.

**Example.** “The consequences of your behavior may include expulsion”—expulsion is not a consequence but an imposed punishment

**Diagnostic question.** Is this a natural consequence or an imposed decision? Could someone have decided otherwise?

**Psychological source.** The wish to shed responsibility for a decision

**Related fallacies:** 2.2.1, [[Appeal to Nature]]
