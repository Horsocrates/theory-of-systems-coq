**2.3.5.1** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.3 Domain 3 Framework Selection|2.3 Framework Selection]] · subtype: Irrelevant Criterion

**Formulation.** The claim that less able, less fortunate, or more trusting people “naturally” deserve to be exploited

**Mechanism of the violation.** The framework of evaluation is chosen by an irrelevant criterion: ability/strength/luck is presented as a moral ground for the distribution of goods or for a right to exploit.

**Example.** “Life is hard, you have to be tough. If he let himself be cheated, it’s his own fault”

**Diagnostic question.** Is ability/strength a relevant criterion for this moral judgment?

**Psychological source.** Just-world hypothesis, self-justification

**Related fallacies:** [[Appeal to Nature]], 2.3.3
