**2.5.4.6** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.5 Domain 5 Inference|2.5 Inference]] · subtype: Scale Error / Exceeding the Grounds

**Formulation.** A broad generalization dismisses particular cases; or single examples are extended to everything

**Mechanism of the violation.** The conclusion exceeds its grounds: the general does not license ignoring the particular; the particular does not yield the whole. Includes Pars pro Toto.

**Example.** “One courier was late—all couriers are unreliable”; the first three swans are white—“all swans are white”

**Diagnostic question.** Is the generalization justified? Is important specificity being lost?

**Psychological source.** Cognitive shortcuts, stereotyping

**Related fallacies:** 2.5.4, [[Zero Tolerance]]
