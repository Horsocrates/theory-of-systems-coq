**2.3.1.2** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.3 Domain 3 Framework Selection|2.3 Framework Selection]] · subtype: Category Mismatch

**Formulation.** If something cannot be measured, it does not exist or does not deserve consideration

**Mechanism of the violation.** The framework of science/mathematics is applied to phenomena that do not yield to it. Measurability becomes the criterion of existence.

**Example.** The inflation calculation leaves out gasoline because its price is “too volatile”

**Diagnostic question.** Is measurability an appropriate criterion? Are the data distorted?

**Psychological source.** Scientism, quantomania

**Related fallacies:** [[Lying with Statistics]], [[Procrustean Fallacy]]
