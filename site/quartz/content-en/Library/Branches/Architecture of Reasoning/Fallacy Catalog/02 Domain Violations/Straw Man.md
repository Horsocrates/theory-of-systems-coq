**2.1.1.2** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.1 Domain 1 Recognition|2.1 Recognition]] · subtype: Object Deformation (A→A')

**Formulation.** Creating a weak parody of the opponent’s argument in order to refute it easily

**Mechanism of the violation.** Deformation: the real argument is replaced by a caricature. What is defeated is not the position but its distortion.

**Example.** “Vegetarians talk about feelings—ever seen a cow poring over Shakespeare?”; “Supporters of remote work want to wreck the company”

**Diagnostic question.** Am I representing the opponent’s position accurately? Would they recognize themselves?

**Psychological source.** Intellectual laziness, rhetorical shortcut

**Related fallacies:** [[Red Herring]], [[Olfactory Rhetoric]]
