**2.4.3.2** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.4 Domain 4 Comparison|2.4 Comparison]] · subtype: Unstable Criteria

**Formulation.** Demanding a certain proof and, when it is provided, demanding a different or a greater one

**Mechanism of the violation.** The criteria shift: the standard of success changes after it has been met. The goal is unattainable because it keeps being moved further away.

**Example.** “Prove X” → [X is proved] → “Now prove Y, or it doesn’t count”

**Diagnostic question.** Did the criterion change after it was met? Is the standard attainable in principle?

**Psychological source.** Unwillingness to admit defeat, motivated reasoning

**Related fallacies:** [[Fundamental Attribution Error]], [[Moral Superiority]]
