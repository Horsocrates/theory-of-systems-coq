**2.2.1.5** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.2 Domain 2 Clarification|2.2 Clarification]] · subtype: Meaning Drift

**Formulation.** Everyone is above average or extraordinary; all are heroes, all are winners

**Mechanism of the violation.** Meaning drift: the words “hero” and “winner” lose their sense when applied universally. If everyone is a hero, the word no longer means anything. Inflation of a concept.

**Example.** Every participant in the science fair gets a prize; every soldier is a national hero

**Diagnostic question.** Does the word keep its meaning when it is applied this way? If everyone is X, what does X mean?

**Psychological source.** Unwillingness to upset, false kindness

**Related fallacies:** [[Hero Busting|Hero-Busting]], 1.B.2
