**2.6.4.7** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.6 Domain 6 Reflection|2.6 Reflection]] · subtype: Immunization from Testing

**Formulation.** Ending discussion of one’s actions by declaring them a “private matter”

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reflection is blocked by false privacy: criticism is dismissed as an intrusion into personal space.

**Example.** “Yes, I was going 80 miles an hour—what’s it to you? You’re not a cop”; “Yes, I broke the rules. So what? None of your business”

**Diagnostic question.** Is this a private matter, or does it affect others?

**Psychological source.** Avoidance of responsibility, defensive autonomy

**Related fallacies:** 2.6.4, Taboo
