**2.1.2.11** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.1 Domain 1 Recognition|2.1 Recognition]] · subtype: Object Substitution (A→B)

**Formulation.** “That’s what Hitler would say” as a refutation of a position

**Mechanism of the violation.** Object substitution: instead of an evaluation of the argument, an association with Hitler. A form of Guilt by Association.

**Example.** “Hitler was a vegetarian—so vegetarianism is bad”

**Diagnostic question.** Does an association with Hitler refute the argument itself?

**Psychological source.** Emotional shortcut, historical trauma

**Related fallacies:** [[Ad Hominem Argument]], [[Guilt by Association]]
