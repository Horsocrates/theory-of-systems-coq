**2.5.2.4** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.5 Domain 5 Inference|2.5 Inference]] · subtype: Causal Error

**Formulation.** “Positive thinking” by itself bends reality in our favor before any action

**Mechanism of the violation.** False causality: thoughts → reality without a mechanism. Part of a cultlike ideology that bans “negative” facts.

**Example.** “Visualize success—and it will come”; “the Law of Attraction”; a refusal to discuss risks

**Diagnostic question.** Is there a mechanism connecting my thoughts with external events?

**Psychological source.** Wishful thinking, illusion of control

**Related fallacies:** [[Magical Thinking]], [[Deliberate Ignorance]]
