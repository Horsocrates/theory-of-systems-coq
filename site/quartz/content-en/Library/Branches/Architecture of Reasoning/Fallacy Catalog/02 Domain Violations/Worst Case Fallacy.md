**2.5.4.10** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.5 Domain 5 Inference|2.5 Inference]] · subtype: Scale Error / Exceeding the Grounds

**Formulation.** Reasoning on the basis of an improbable worst-case scenario

**Mechanism of the violation.** A conclusion from an unrealistic premise: the worst-case scenario is taken to be probable.

**Example.** “What if a meteorite hits the warehouse? Let’s prepare for everything at once!”

**Diagnostic question.** How realistic is the “worst case”?

**Psychological source.** Catastrophizing, fear

**Related fallacies:** [[Scare Tactics|Scare Tactics / Appeal to Fear]], [[Positive Thinking Fallacy]]
