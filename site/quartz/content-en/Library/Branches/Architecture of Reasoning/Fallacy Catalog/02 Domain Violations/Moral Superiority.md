**2.3.6.3** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.3 Domain 3 Framework Selection|2.3 Framework Selection]] · subtype: Framework for Result

**Formulation.** Evil has no rights that Good is bound to respect; against the enemy everything is permitted

**Mechanism of the violation.** The framework of “righteousness” removes moral constraints: since we are right and they are evil, nothing is owed to them. A denial of the Golden Rule.

**Example.** The Inquisition, torture—against heretics everything is permitted; “no negotiations with evil”

**Diagnostic question.** Do I apply the same moral standards to “us” and to “them”?

**Psychological source.** Moral disengagement, dehumanization

**Related fallacies:** [[Appeal to Heaven]], [[Moving the Goalposts]]
