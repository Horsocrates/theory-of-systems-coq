**2.3.6.4** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.3 Domain 3 Framework Selection|2.3 Framework Selection]] · subtype: Framework for Result

**Formulation.** Declaring an “emergency” in order to give a disproportionate response

**Mechanism of the violation.** The framework of “emergency” justifies ignoring justice and proportionality.

**Example.** “One case of cannibalism—we need a federal program with a million-dollar budget”

**Diagnostic question.** Is the response proportionate to the scale of the problem?

**Psychological source.** Moral panic, political exploitation

**Related fallacies:** [[We Have to Do Something]], Dog Whistle, [[Big Lie Technique]]
