**2.5.4.8** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.5 Domain 5 Inference|2.5 Inference]] · subtype: Scale Error / Exceeding the Grounds

**Formulation.** A hasty conclusion or action without sufficient evidence

**Mechanism of the violation.** The conclusion exceeds its grounds: a strong conclusion is drawn from weak signs. “Looks like” ≠ “is.”

**Example.** “There are rumors about him—there must be something to them”

**Diagnostic question.** Is there enough evidence for the conclusion?

**Psychological source.** Jumping to conclusions, prejudice

**Related fallacies:** [[Paralysis of Analysis|Paralysis of Analysis / Nirvana Fallacy]], Just in Case
