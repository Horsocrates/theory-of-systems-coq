**2.1.2.14** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.1 Domain 1 Recognition|2.1 Recognition]] · subtype: Object Substitution (A→B)

**Formulation.** Evaluating an argument by its emotional tone rather than by its content

**Mechanism of the violation.** Object substitution: instead of the argument, the tone is evaluated. A valid argument remains valid regardless of its delivery.

**Example.** “I won’t discuss this until you calm down”; “Too many exclamation marks”

**Diagnostic question.** Am I evaluating the content or the manner of delivery?

**Psychological source.** Deflection, power dynamics

**Related fallacies:** [[Ad Hominem Argument]], [[Paternalism]]
