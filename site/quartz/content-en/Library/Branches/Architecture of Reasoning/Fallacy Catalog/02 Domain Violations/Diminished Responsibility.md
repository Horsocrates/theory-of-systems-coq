**2.2.1.2** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.2 Domain 2 Clarification|2.2 Clarification]] · subtype: Meaning Drift

**Formulation.** Applying a legal concept (mitigation of punishment) to reality as a whole

**Mechanism of the violation.** Meaning drift: “diminished responsibility” in the legal sense (a lesser punishment) turns into “the act does not count.” But the consequences are real regardless of the state of the one who acted.

**Example.** “Yes, I was speeding and killed a man, but I was drunk, so it’s not so bad”

**Diagnostic question.** Does my state affect the reality of the consequences or only the measure of punishment?

**Psychological source.** Self-justification, avoidance of responsibility

**Related fallacies:** [[Actions Have Consequences]]
