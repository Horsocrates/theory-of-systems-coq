**2.6.4.4** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.6 Domain 6 Reflection|2.6 Reflection]] · subtype: Immunization from Testing

**Formulation.** An action or position cannot be questioned because “there is a job to be done”

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reflection is blocked: the very fact of a “task” excludes discussing it. “It is not for us to reason; it is for us to carry out.” The task becomes part of one’s identity.

**Example.** “Ours is not to reason why, ours is but to do or die”; “It’s just a job”—as a justification

**Diagnostic question.** Is a “task” a ground for not asking questions about it?

**Psychological source.** Work identity, avoidance of moral responsibility

**Related fallacies:** 1.B.12, [[Argument from Inertia]]
