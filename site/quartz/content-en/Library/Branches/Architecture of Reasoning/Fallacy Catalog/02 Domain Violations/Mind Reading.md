**2.1.4.1** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.1 Domain 1 Recognition|2.1 Recognition]] · subtype: Projection

**Formulation.** Speculation about another person’s thoughts, emotions, and motives with a claim to exact knowledge

**Mechanism of the violation.** Projection: one’s own interpretations of another’s thoughts are taken for fact. Speculation is passed off as knowledge.

**Example.** “The boss doesn’t really care about the team”—a groundless claim about someone else’s motives

**Diagnostic question.** How do I know what this person thinks? Is this a fact or my interpretation?

**Psychological source.** Overconfidence, projection

**Related fallacies:** 2.1.4, [[Argument from Motives]]
