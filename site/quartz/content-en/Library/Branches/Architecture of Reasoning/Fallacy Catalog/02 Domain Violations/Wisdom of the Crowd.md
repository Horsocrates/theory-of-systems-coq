**2.5.4.9** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.5 Domain 5 Inference|2.5 Inference]] · subtype: Scale Error / Exceeding the Grounds

**Formulation.** Individuals err, but “the crowd” or “the market” is infallible

**Mechanism of the violation.** A false ground: aggregation of opinions ≠ absolute truth. The crowd burned witches; the market crashed in 2008.

**Example.** “The market is always right”; trusting Wikipedia as the final authority

**Diagnostic question.** Does mass agreement guarantee that something is true?

**Psychological source.** Social proof, diffusion of responsibility

**Related fallacies:** Bandwagon, 2.5.4
