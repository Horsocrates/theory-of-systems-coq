**2.3.6.2** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.3 Domain 3 Framework Selection|2.3 Framework Selection]] · subtype: Framework for Result

**Formulation.** Past good behavior or suffering gives the right to an immoral act without consequences

**Mechanism of the violation.** The framework of “deservingness” replaces moral evaluation. Past merits do not cancel the moral status of the present action.

**Example.** “I’ve been good all year, so one bad deed doesn’t count”

**Diagnostic question.** Do past merits cancel the moral status of the present action?

**Psychological source.** Self-licensing, entitlement

**Related fallacies:** [[Argument from Motives]], [[Big but Fallacy|Big ‘But’ Fallacy]]
