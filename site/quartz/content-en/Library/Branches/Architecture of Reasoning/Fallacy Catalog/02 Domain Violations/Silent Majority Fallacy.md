**2.5.4.7** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.5 Domain 5 Inference|2.5 Inference]] · subtype: Scale Error / Exceeding the Grounds

**Formulation.** A claim to know about a hidden “silent majority” contrary to the polls

**Mechanism of the violation.** A conclusion without grounds: the “silent majority” is postulated without evidence. A variant of Argument from Ignorance.

**Example.** “Everyone who keeps silent is for me: they are the majority”; the leader as “the voice of the voiceless”

**Diagnostic question.** Is there evidence that this “majority” exists?

**Psychological source.** Wishful thinking, false consensus

**Related fallacies:** [[Argument from Ignorance]], 2.5.4
