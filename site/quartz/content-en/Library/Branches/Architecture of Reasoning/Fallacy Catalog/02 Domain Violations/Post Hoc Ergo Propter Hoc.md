**2.5.2.5** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.5 Domain 5 Inference|2.5 Inference]] · subtype: Causal Error

**Formulation.** If B came after A, then A caused B—attributing causality to coincidences

**Mechanism of the violation.** False causality: temporal sequence ≠ causal connection. Correlation ≠ causation.

**Example.** “The rooster crowed—and the sun rose: so the rooster raised it!”

**Diagnostic question.** Is there a causal mechanism? Or only a coincidence in time?

**Psychological source.** Pattern recognition, apophenia

**Related fallacies:** 2.5.2, Clustering Illusion
