**2.2.1.7** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.2 Domain 2 Clarification|2.2 Clarification]] · subtype: Meaning Drift

**Formulation.** Treating intellectual categories as real “things”

**Mechanism of the violation.** An abstraction is taken for reality: a category becomes a “thing.” The map is confused with the territory.

**Example.** “The market has decided”; “Science says”—abstractions taken as agents

**Diagnostic question.** Is this a real entity or an intellectual category?

**Psychological source.** Abstraction confusion

**Related fallacies:** [[Essentializing]], 2.2.1
