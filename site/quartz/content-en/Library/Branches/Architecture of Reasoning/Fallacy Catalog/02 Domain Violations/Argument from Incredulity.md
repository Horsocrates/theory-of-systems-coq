**2.6.4.1** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.6 Domain 6 Reflection|2.6 Reflection]] · subtype: Immunization from Testing

**Formulation.** Rejecting a claim only because it seems “incredible” or contradicts personal experience

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reflection refuses consideration: “I can’t imagine this” becomes a ground for not considering the argument at all. A subjective inability to imagine is used as a shield against testing.

**Example.** Brushing off a scientific argument: “That’s insane!”—without considering it on its merits

**Diagnostic question.** Is my inability to imagine X evidence against X, or a refusal to consider it?

**Psychological source.** The representativeness heuristic, limited experience

**Related fallacies:** [[Affective Fallacy Follow Your Heart|Affective Fallacy / Follow Your Heart]]
