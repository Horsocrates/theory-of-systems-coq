*Affective Fallacy*

**Formulation.** Emotions are declared self-validating and not subject to criticism: “I feel it—so it is the truth”

**Mechanism of the violation.** The framework of evaluation: “feeling = truth.” An emotion is taken as the criterion of truth-value, although emotion and truth-value are different categories. In addition: immunization from criticism.

**Example.** “I feel that it’s true, so it’s true. My feelings are valid; you can’t dispute them”

**Diagnostic question.** Is my feeling a ground for the truth-value of the claim? Is the claim subject to testing?

**Psychological source.** Emotional reasoning

**Related fallacies:** 1.B.3, 2.6.4
