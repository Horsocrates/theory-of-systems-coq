**2.1.2.15** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.1 Domain 1 Recognition|2.1 Recognition]] · subtype: Object Substitution (A→B)

**Formulation.** A false association of a famous person or symbol with an unrelated object

**Mechanism of the violation.** Object substitution: the evaluation is transferred from an authority to an unrelated object. MLK in a mattress ad.

**Example.** A photo of MLK in a mattress ad; the Texas flag for cars from Detroit; Genghis Khan for a Chinese restaurant

**Diagnostic question.** Is there a real connection between the authority and the object?

**Psychological source.** Halo effect, borrowed credibility

**Related fallacies:** [[Star Power|Star Power / Testimonial]], [[Guilt by Association]]
