**2.5.2.2** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.5 Domain 5 Inference|2.5 Inference]] · subtype: Causal Error

**Formulation.** With enough faith, the right words, or rituals, the laws of the universe will be suspended

**Mechanism of the violation.** False causality: faith/ritual → the desired result. A causal connection is postulated without a mechanism. A denial of a predictable universe.

**Example.** “Make your wish the right way—and the universe will hear”; a ritual before an exam instead of preparation

**Diagnostic question.** Is there a causal mechanism between my actions and the expected result?

**Psychological source.** Illusion of control, wishful thinking

**Related fallacies:** [[Appeal to Heaven]], [[Jobs Comforter Fallacy|Job’s Comforter Fallacy]]
