**2.2.3.3** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.2 Domain 2 Clarification|2.2 Clarification]] · subtype: Incomplete Analysis

**Formulation.** Simple answers or slogans for complex questions

**Mechanism of the violation.** Incomplete clarification: complexity is reduced to a slogan. The simplification deceives.

**Example.** “If it doesn’t fit, you must acquit”; “Vote for Smith—he’ll bring back the jobs!”

**Diagnostic question.** Does the simplicity of the answer match the complexity of the question?

**Psychological source.** Cognitive ease, appeal to simplicity

**Related fallacies:** [[Plain Truth Fallacy|Plain Truth Fallacy / KISS Principle]], Dog Whistle
