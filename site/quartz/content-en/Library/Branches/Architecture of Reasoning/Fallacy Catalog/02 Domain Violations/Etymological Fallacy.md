**2.2.1.4** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.2 Domain 2 Clarification|2.2 Clarification]] · subtype: Meaning Drift

**Formulation.** False conclusions drawn from the origin of a word or from its meaning in another language

**Mechanism of the violation.** The meaning of a word in current usage is replaced by its etymology. Words change their meaning; origin does not determine present sense.

**Example.** “I won’t eat fish! The French ‘poisson’ looks like the English ‘poison’—that must mean something!”

**Diagnostic question.** Is the origin of the word relevant to its current meaning?

**Psychological source.** Magical thinking about words

**Related fallacies:** 2.2.1, [[Appeal to Tradition]]
