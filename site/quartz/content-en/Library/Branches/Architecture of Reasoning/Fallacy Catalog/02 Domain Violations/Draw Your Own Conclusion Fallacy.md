**2.5.4.4** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.5 Domain 5 Inference|2.5 Inference]] · subtype: Scale Error / Exceeding the Grounds

**Formulation.** Presenting selected “shocking facts” and inviting the audience to “draw its own conclusions”

**Mechanism of the violation.** The conclusion is not formally drawn but is provoked. The data are selected so that the conclusion seems “obvious.” The audience draws a conclusion that exceeds the ground but feels it to be “its own.”

**Example.** “That department missed its deadlines again—draw your own conclusions”

**Diagnostic question.** What conclusion am I being nudged toward? Are there enough data for this conclusion?

**Psychological source.** The illusion of independent discovery

**Related fallacies:** 2.1.3, 1.A.2
