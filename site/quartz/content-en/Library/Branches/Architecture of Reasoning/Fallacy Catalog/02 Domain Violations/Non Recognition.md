**2.6.4.8** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.6 Domain 6 Reflection|2.6 Reflection]] · subtype: Immunization from Testing

**Formulation.** Refusing to acknowledge an obvious reality on the grounds that acknowledging it would “reward” the villains

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reflection is blocked by ideology: facts are not acknowledged so as not to “legitimize” the enemy. Reality does not disappear when it is not acknowledged.

**Example.** For years a firm does not name its competitor in its reports—as if the competitor did not exist

**Diagnostic question.** Does non-recognition change reality? Or only my interaction with it?

**Psychological source.** Wishful thinking, ideological rigidity

**Related fallacies:** [[Deliberate Ignorance]], [[NIMBY Fallacy]]
