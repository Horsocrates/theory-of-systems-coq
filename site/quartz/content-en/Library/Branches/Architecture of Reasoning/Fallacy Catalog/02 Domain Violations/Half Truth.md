**2.1.3.3** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.1 Domain 1 Recognition|2.1 Recognition]] · subtype: Data Filtering

**Formulation.** Deliberately selecting only the information that supports a position while keeping silent about key details

**Mechanism of the violation.** Filtering: each separate fact is true, but the whole is false because of deliberate omissions. The truth is told, but what matters is hidden.

**Example.** An apartment listing: “sunny side, quiet neighbors”—with nothing said about the highway under the windows

**Diagnostic question.** What information might have been left out? Would it change the picture?

**Psychological source.** The wish to persuade, selective presentation

**Related fallacies:** [[Availability Bias]], 4.1
