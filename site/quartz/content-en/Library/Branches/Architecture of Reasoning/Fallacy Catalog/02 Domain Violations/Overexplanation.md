**2.2.4.1** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.2 Domain 2 Clarification|2.2 Clarification]] · subtype: Excessive Analysis

**Formulation.** Beyond a certain point, more explanation leads to less understanding

**Mechanism of the violation.** The paradox of clarification: an excess of detail obscures instead of clarifying.

**Example.** “Mansplaining”—the urge to be the smartest person in the room regardless of actual knowledge

**Diagnostic question.** Does the additional explanation help understanding, or does it confuse?

**Psychological source.** Need to be right, status display

**Related fallacies:** Snow Job, Plain Truth Fallacy
