**2.1.4.2** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.1 Domain 1 Recognition|2.1 Recognition]] · subtype: Projection

**Formulation.** The automatic assumption that everyone shares our desires, interests, and morals

**Mechanism of the violation.** Projection: one’s own values are attributed to others without checking. It denies the reality of differences and the capacity for evil.

**Example.** “Surely everyone wants the same thing we do”—one’s own motives projected onto the other side of a negotiation

**Diagnostic question.** How do I know they want the same thing? Have I checked?

**Psychological source.** False consensus effect, projection

**Related fallacies:** [[Othering]], [[Mind Reading|Mind-Reading]]
