**2.1.2.16** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.1 Domain 1 Recognition|2.1 Recognition]] · subtype: Object Substitution (A→B)

**Formulation.** Defending a position by pointing out that the opponent does the same or worse

**Mechanism of the violation.** Object substitution: instead of defending the position, an attack on the opponent. “You too” does not make an action right.

**Example.** “Yes, I was late—but you didn’t show up at all yesterday!”; “Your own report was full of mistakes!”

**Diagnostic question.** Does the opponent’s fault make my position right?

**Psychological source.** Deflection, moral relativism

**Related fallacies:** [[Red Herring]], [[Ad Hominem Argument]]
