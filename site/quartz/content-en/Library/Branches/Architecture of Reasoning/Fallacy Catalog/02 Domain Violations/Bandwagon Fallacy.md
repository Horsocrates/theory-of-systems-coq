**2.3.5.5** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.3 Domain 3 Framework Selection|2.3 Framework Selection]] · subtype: Irrelevant Criterion

**Formulation.** “Everyone thinks/does so” as an argument that something is true or right

**Mechanism of the violation.** The framework of evaluation: “popularity = truth-value.” The number of believers is unrelated to the truth-value of a claim. Millions of people believed that the Earth was flat.

**Example.** “75% of Americans think Bob Hodiak is a thief. That proves he should be locked up!”

**Diagnostic question.** Is the number of believers proof that a claim is true?

**Psychological source.** Conformity bias, social proof

**Related fallacies:** [[Appeal to Tradition]], [[Alternative Truth]], 1.B.1
