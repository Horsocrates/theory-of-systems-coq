**2.1.2.3** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.1 Domain 1 Recognition|2.1 Recognition]] · subtype: Object Substitution (A→B)

**Formulation.** A claim is automatically considered true because it was made by a relative, a friend, or “one of us”

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reverse ad hominem: instead of the argument (A), one’s relationship to the speaker (B) is evaluated. “He’s one of us, so he’s right”—a relationship does not determine truth-value.

**Example.** “My brother-in-law says he saw you goofing off. You’re fired!”—without checking the claim

**Diagnostic question.** Am I evaluating the claim on its merits or by who made it?

**Psychological source.** Tribalism, in-group bias

**Related fallacies:** [[Ad Hominem Argument]]
