**2.4.3.1** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.4 Domain 4 Comparison|2.4 Comparison]] · subtype: Unstable Criteria

**Formulation.** The bad behavior of others is explained by their character, one’s own by circumstances

**Mechanism of the violation.** The criteria of evaluation are unstable: for oneself a situational explanation is applied, for others a dispositional one. A double standard in the attribution of causes.

**Example.** “I get up at 10 a.m. because the neighbors make noise until 2 a.m. You get up at 10 because you’re lazy”

**Diagnostic question.** Do I apply the same criteria to myself and to others?

**Psychological source.** Fundamental attribution error, self-serving bias

**Related fallacies:** 2.4.3, [[Defensiveness]]
