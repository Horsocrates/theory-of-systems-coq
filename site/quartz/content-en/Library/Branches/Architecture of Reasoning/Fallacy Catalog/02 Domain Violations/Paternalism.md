**2.1.2.10** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.1 Domain 1 Recognition|2.1 Recognition]] · subtype: Object Substitution (A→B)

**Formulation.** Condescending dismissal of the opponent’s arguments as “childish” or “immature”

**Mechanism of the violation.** Object substitution: instead of the arguments, the opponent’s “maturity” is evaluated. “Your argument is infantile” is not an answer.

**Example.** “Your argument against the war is childish. Try again like an adult”; “Somebody has to be the adult in the room”

**Diagnostic question.** Am I responding to the content or to the opponent’s “maturity”?

**Psychological source.** Condescension, power dynamics

**Related fallacies:** [[Ad Hominem Argument]], [[Tone Policing]]
