**2.4.5.1** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.4 Domain 4 Comparison|2.4 Comparison]] · subtype: Comparison with Nonexistent

**Formulation.** Since no one is perfect, there are no heroes—any flaw disqualifies

**Mechanism of the violation.** A real person is compared with an unattainable ideal. Any deviation from perfection is used for complete disqualification.

**Example.** “Every master craftsman turns out a defective piece now and then—so there are no masters”

**Diagnostic question.** Am I comparing a real person with an attainable standard or with an ideal?

**Psychological source.** Cynicism, disillusionment

**Related fallacies:** 2.4.5, [[Either or Reasoning|Either/Or Reasoning]]
