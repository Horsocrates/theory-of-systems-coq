**2.2.1.6** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.2 Domain 2 Clarification|2.2 Clarification]] · subtype: Meaning Drift

**Formulation.** The assumption that the nature of a thing can be changed by changing its name

**Mechanism of the violation.** Substitution through renaming: a change of term creates the illusion of a solution. “Victims” → “survivors”—the perpetrators disappear.

**Example.** “Pets” → “animal companions”; “victims” → “survivors” (the perpetrators disappear)

**Diagnostic question.** Does renaming change reality? Or only perception?

**Psychological source.** Magical thinking about language

**Related fallacies:** [[Passive Voice Fallacy]], [[Non Recognition|Non-Recognition]]
