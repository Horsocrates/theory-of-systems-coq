**2.6.4.2** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.6 Domain 6 Reflection|2.6 Reflection]] · subtype: Immunization from Testing

**Formulation.** Dismissing well-grounded objections as “cards” in a rhetorical game

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reflection refuses consideration: valid objections are disqualified in advance as a “trick” or a “card.” Instead of an answer to the argument—a meta-accusation of manipulation.

**Example.** “You’re just playing on pity”—in response to a real complaint; an accusation of playing a “card” instead of an analysis

**Diagnostic question.** Have I considered the objection on its merits or dismissed it as a “trick”?

**Psychological source.** Defense of one’s position, avoidance of inconvenient arguments

**Related fallacies:** [[Argument from Incredulity]], [[Ad Hominem Argument]]
