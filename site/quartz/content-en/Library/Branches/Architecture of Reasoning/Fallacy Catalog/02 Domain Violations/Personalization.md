**2.5.2.3** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.5 Domain 5 Inference|2.5 Inference]] · subtype: Causal Error

**Formulation.** Seeing oneself as the cause of an external event for which one bears no responsibility

**Mechanism of the violation.** False causality: one attributes to oneself a role in events that have nothing to do with oneself. There is no causal mechanism.

**Example.** “It’s my bad luck—a blizzard on the very day of our festival. If it weren’t for me, it wouldn’t have happened!”

**Diagnostic question.** Is there a real mechanism by which I influence this event?

**Psychological source.** Illusion of control, narcissism

**Related fallacies:** [[Jobs Comforter Fallacy|Job’s Comforter Fallacy]], [[Magical Thinking]]
