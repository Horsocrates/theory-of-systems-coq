**2.1.2.5** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.1 Domain 1 Recognition|2.1 Recognition]] · subtype: Object Substitution (A→B)

**Formulation.** The validity of arguments depends not on their strength but on the identity (gender, race, group) of the one who makes them

**Mechanism of the violation.** Object substitution: instead of the argument (A), the speaker’s identity (B) is evaluated. Arguments are rejected without being considered on their merits.

**Example.** “You’d understand if you worked on our shop floor, and since you don’t, there’s no point explaining”

**Diagnostic question.** Does the speaker’s identity determine the truth-value of their arguments?

**Psychological source.** Tribalism, in-group/out-group

**Related fallacies:** [[Ad Hominem Argument]], [[Guilt by Association]], [[Othering]]
