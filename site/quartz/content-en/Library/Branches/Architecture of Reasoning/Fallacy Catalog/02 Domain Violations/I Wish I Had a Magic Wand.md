**2.6.4.6** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.6 Domain 6 Reflection|2.6 Reflection]] · subtype: Immunization from Testing

**Formulation.** A false claim of one’s own powerlessness to change a situation over which one has control

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reflection is blocked by false helplessness: “I can’t do anything” closes the discussion of alternatives. In fact the power is there.

**Example.** “What can be done about the expenses? As the director, I wish I had a magic wand...”

**Diagnostic question.** Is there really no way to exert influence? Or is this a way of avoiding responsibility?

**Psychological source.** Avoidance of responsibility, learned helplessness

**Related fallacies:** TINA, [[Default Bias]]
