**2.1.5.1** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.1 Domain 1 Recognition|2.1 Recognition]] · subtype: Source Distortion

**Formulation.** The fantasy that the enemy can instantly “radicalize” an audience with its irresistibly persuasive “propaganda”

**Mechanism of the violation.** Source distortion: the opponent’s ordinary communication is distorted into “magically persuasive propaganda.” This makes it possible not to consider the arguments on their merits.

**Example.** “Don’t look at that website! They’re trying to brainwash you with their propaganda!”

**Diagnostic question.** Is “brainwashing” a description of a real mechanism or a way of not considering the arguments?

**Psychological source.** Fear, tribalism, projection

**Related fallacies:** 2.1.1, 1.B.6
