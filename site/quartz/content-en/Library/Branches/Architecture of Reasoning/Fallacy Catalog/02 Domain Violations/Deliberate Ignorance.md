**2.6.4.3** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.6 Domain 6 Reflection|2.6 Reflection]] · subtype: Immunization from Testing

**Formulation.** A conscious choice not to listen, to “tune out” any information that challenges one’s beliefs

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reflection is actively blocked: instead of considering counterarguments—a refusal to take them in. “Don’t confuse me with the facts, my mind is made up!”

**Example.** “I don’t want to hear it!”; the three monkeys: see no evil, hear no evil, speak no evil

**Diagnostic question.** Am I avoiding information that could call my beliefs into question?

**Psychological source.** Avoidance of cognitive dissonance

**Related fallacies:** [[Argument from Incredulity]], 4.1
