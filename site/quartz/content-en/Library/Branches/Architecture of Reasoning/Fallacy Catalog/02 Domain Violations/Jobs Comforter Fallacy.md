**2.5.2.1** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.5 Domain 5 Inference|2.5 Inference]] · subtype: Causal Error

**Formulation.** Every misfortune must be a punishment for someone’s sin—there is no such thing as chance

**Mechanism of the violation.** False causality: guilt is inferred from misfortune. “Something bad happened → someone sinned.” A causal connection is postulated without a mechanism.

**Example.** “It’s his own fault—trouble doesn’t come to those who live right”: suffering read as a verdict

**Diagnostic question.** Is there a real causal connection between the “sin” and the misfortune?

**Psychological source.** Just-world hypothesis

**Related fallacies:** [[Appeal to Heaven]], [[Magical Thinking]]
