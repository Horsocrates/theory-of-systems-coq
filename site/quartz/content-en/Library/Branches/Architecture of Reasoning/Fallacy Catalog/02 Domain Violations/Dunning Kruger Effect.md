**2.6.2.1** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.6 Domain 6 Reflection|2.6 Reflection]] · subtype: Self-Assessment Distortion

**Formulation.** A cognitive bias in which people with limited knowledge overestimate their abilities

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reflection is defective: there is not enough knowledge to assess one’s own ignorance. “I don’t know what I don’t know”—and therefore I think I know everything. Meta-ignorance.

**Example.** “I know that Washington never told a lie and Lincoln freed the slaves—why do I need a history course? I already know everything!”

**Diagnostic question.** Do I know enough to assess how much I don’t know?

**Psychological source.** Dunning-Kruger effect

**Related fallacies:** 2.6.1, 2.6.4
