**2.1.1.1** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.1 Domain 1 Recognition|2.1 Recognition]] · subtype: Object Deformation (A→A')

**Formulation.** An irrelevant argument that diverts attention with an emotionally charged but unrelated topic

**Mechanism of the violation.** Substitution of the object: instead of the topic (A), another one (A') is brought in—emotional but unrelated.

**Example.** “About my report—but have you seen what’s going on next door?”—the conversation is led away

**Diagnostic question.** Is this topic connected to the question under discussion?

**Psychological source.** Deflection, attention manipulation

**Related fallacies:** [[Availability Bias]], Dog Whistle
