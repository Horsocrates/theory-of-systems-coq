**2.4.1.2** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.4 Domain 4 Comparison|2.4 Comparison]] · subtype: False Equation

**Formulation.** The inappropriate application of sports imagery to war or intimacy

**Mechanism of the violation.** False analogy: war or intimacy is not a game with “points.” The sports framework hides the real stakes.

**Example.** “We crushed our competitors in the final round of the negotiations”—sports vocabulary where there is no sport

**Diagnostic question.** Is the sports metaphor appropriate? What does it hide?

**Psychological source.** Machismo, trivialization

**Related fallacies:** [[False Analogy]], 2.3.1
