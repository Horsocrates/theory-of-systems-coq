**2.2.1.3** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.2 Domain 2 Clarification|2.2 Clarification]] · subtype: Meaning Drift

**Formulation.** Deliberately using words in a sense other than the one the audience will understand, or refusing to define terms

**Mechanism of the violation.** A term is deliberately used in two meanings: the speaker means one thing, the audience understands another. Formally he “did not lie”; in fact he deceived.

**Example.** “Nothing is better than absolute truth; a sandwich is better than nothing—so a sandwich is better than absolute truth”—“nothing” changes its sense in the middle of the argument

**Diagnostic question.** In exactly what sense is this word used? Does my understanding coincide with the speaker’s intention?

**Psychological source.** The wish to deceive without a formal lie

**Related fallacies:** [[Actions Have Consequences]], 2.2.2
