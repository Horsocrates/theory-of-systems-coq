**2.3.1.3** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.3 Domain 3 Framework Selection|2.3 Framework Selection]] · subtype: Category Mismatch

**Formulation.** Applying norms of standardization to free human beings who are inherently diverse

**Mechanism of the violation.** The framework of industrial standardization applied to people: diversity is forced into a single template. People are not machine parts.

**Example.** Marching in formation, uniforms, the goose step; “objective” evaluation of everyone by a template

**Diagnostic question.** Is standardization appropriate here? Does it destroy essential diversity?

**Psychological source.** Need for control, order obsession

**Related fallacies:** [[Measurability Fallacy]], [[Essentializing]]
