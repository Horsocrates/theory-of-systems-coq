**2.6.1.5** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.6 Domain 6 Reflection|2.6 Reflection]] · subtype: Illusion of Completion

**Formulation.** Any claim about something “new” is rejected a priori—all this has happened before

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reflection is closed by cynicism: the new is reduced to the old without analysis. False uniformitarianism.

**Example.** “Libertarianism is warmed-over anarchism, which is an ancient heresy. Nothing is new”

**Diagnostic question.** Is this really “the same thing”? Or are there essential differences?

**Psychological source.** Cynicism, intellectual fatigue

**Related fallacies:** [[Law of Unintended Consequences]], [[Cost Bias]]
