**2.1.2.6** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.1 Domain 1 Recognition|2.1 Recognition]] · subtype: Object Substitution (A→B)

**Formulation.** An argument deserves trust because the speaker is “an ordinary person” who “says what he thinks”

**Mechanism of the violation.** Object substitution: instead of the argument (A), the speaker’s image as “one of us” (B) is evaluated. “He’s a regular guy” does not establish truth-value.

**Example.** A candidate for the residents’ board of an apartment building calls himself “a simple tenant” as opposed to the “smart alecks” and “professors”

**Diagnostic question.** Am I evaluating the arguments on their merits or the speaker’s image?

**Psychological source.** Tribalism, anti-intellectualism

**Related fallacies:** [[Ad Hominem Argument]], [[Identity Fallacy]]
