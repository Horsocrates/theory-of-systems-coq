**2.2.3.1** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.2 Domain 2 Clarification|2.2 Clarification]] · subtype: Incomplete Analysis

**Formulation.** Falsely offering only two options when a wide range of alternatives exists

**Mechanism of the violation.** The space of possibilities has not been explored: out of many options, only the two extremes are presented. Intermediate and alternative options are ignored.

**Example.** “Either you’re 100% with us, or you’re an enemy!”; “Are you for quality or for speed?”—as if no third were given

**Diagnostic question.** Are there really only two options? What other possibilities have I not considered?

**Psychological source.** Cognitive simplicity, polarization

**Related fallacies:** [[Taboo|Taboo / Dogmatism]], 2.5.4
