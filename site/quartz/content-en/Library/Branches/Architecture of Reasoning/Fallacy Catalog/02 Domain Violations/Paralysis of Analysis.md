**2.6.1.6** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.6 Domain 6 Reflection|2.6 Reflection]] · subtype: Illusion of Completion

**Formulation.** Since all the data are never in, no decision can be made

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reflection turns into procrastination: the ideal of complete information makes any decision impossible.

**Example.** Endless postponement “until all the facts are in”; the demand for a perfect solution

**Diagnostic question.** Is there enough information for a reasonable decision?

**Psychological source.** Perfectionism, fear of error

**Related fallacies:** [[Law of Unintended Consequences]], 2.4.5
