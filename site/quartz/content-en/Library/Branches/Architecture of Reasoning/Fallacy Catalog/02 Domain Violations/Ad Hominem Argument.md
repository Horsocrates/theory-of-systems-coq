**2.1.2.1** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.1 Domain 1 Recognition|2.1 Recognition]] · subtype: Object Substitution (A→B)

**Formulation.** Instead of the argument, the person advancing it is attacked

**Mechanism of the violation.** The object of recognition is substituted: instead of the argument (A), the personality of the one arguing (B) is recognized. Attention switches from WHAT was said to WHO said it.

**Example.** “This so-called judge…”; “He is so bad that you can’t believe anything he says”

**Diagnostic question.** What am I considering right now—the argument or the person? Has the argument been considered on its merits?

**Psychological source.** Ego defense, anger, dislike

**Related fallacies:** 1.B.6, 2.1.1
