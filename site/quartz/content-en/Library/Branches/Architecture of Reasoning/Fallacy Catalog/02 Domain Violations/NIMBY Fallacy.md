**2.1.3.5** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.1 Domain 1 Recognition|2.1 Recognition]] · subtype: Data Filtering

**Formulation.** A problem that is not nearby or not visible has “disappeared” and ceased to exist

**Mechanism of the violation.** Filtering: a problem is ignored if it is physically removed or hidden from the media. The problem has not been solved—only moved.

**Example.** “The city needs a landfill—but not on our street”; the problem is not solved but shifted onto others

**Diagnostic question.** Has the problem been solved, or only moved out of sight?

**Psychological source.** Out of sight, out of mind

**Related fallacies:** [[Availability Bias]], [[Deliberate Ignorance]]
