**2.5.3.1** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.5 Domain 5 Inference|2.5 Inference]] · subtype: Chain Error

**Formulation.** If a little X is good, then a lot of X is even better (or if less X is good, then zero X is ideal)

**Mechanism of the violation.** Extrapolation without justification: the link “a little → good” does not extend linearly. Nonlinearity, saturation points, and side effects are ignored.

**Example.** “If an apple a day is good for you, then a diet of nothing but apples is even better!”

**Diagnostic question.** Is the dependence linear? Is there a point of saturation or of reverse effect?

**Psychological source.** Linear thinking, simplification

**Related fallacies:** 2.5.3, [[Big but Fallacy|Big ‘But’ Fallacy]]
