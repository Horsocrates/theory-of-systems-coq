**2.3.5.6** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.3 Domain 3 Framework Selection|2.3 Framework Selection]] · subtype: Irrelevant Criterion

**Formulation.** Evaluating the quality of a thing by its price or by the effort spent on it rather than by its real properties

**Mechanism of the violation.** The framework of evaluation: “expensive/hard-won = valuable.” Price or effort is unrelated to quality or usefulness. An expensive thing can be useless.

**Example.** “I worked hard for this car! Yes, it’s a wreck, but to me it’s better than a millionaire’s limousine”

**Diagnostic question.** Am I evaluating the thing by its real properties or by how much I paid for it?

**Psychological source.** Sunk cost, self-justification

**Related fallacies:** 2.6.3, [[Appeal to Tradition]]
