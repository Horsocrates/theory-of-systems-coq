**2.5.1.1** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.5 Domain 5 Inference|2.5 Inference]] · subtype: Logical Gap

**Formulation.** Offering evidence or conclusions that are logically unconnected with the question under discussion

**Mechanism of the violation.** The conclusion does not follow from the premises: there is no logical connection between A and B, yet B is offered as a consequence of A.

**Example.** “The reason I failed your course is that the government is now printing purple five-dollar bills!”

**Diagnostic question.** Does the conclusion follow from the premises? Is there a logical connection?

**Psychological source.** Loose associations, confused thinking

**Related fallacies:** 2.5.4, [[Magical Thinking]]
