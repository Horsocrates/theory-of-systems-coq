**2.1.2.12** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.1 Domain 1 Recognition|2.1 Recognition]] · subtype: Object Substitution (A→B)

**Formulation.** A claim to be true only because the position opposes the mainstream

**Mechanism of the violation.** Object substitution: instead of justification, a heroic pose. The opposite of Bandwagon.

**Example.** “They used to think the Earth was flat! Now they say H2O. And what about Area 51?”

**Diagnostic question.** Does opposing the mainstream show that the position is true?

**Psychological source.** Contrarianism, identity through opposition

**Related fallacies:** Bandwagon, [[Ad Hominem Argument]]
