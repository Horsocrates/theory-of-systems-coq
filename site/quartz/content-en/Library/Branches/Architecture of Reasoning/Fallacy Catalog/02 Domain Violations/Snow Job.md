**2.2.4.2** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.2 Domain 2 Clarification|2.2 Clarification]] · subtype: Excessive Analysis

**Formulation.** “Proof” by burying the audience under mountains of documents it cannot evaluate

**Mechanism of the violation.** Excess obscures: the information is true but only marginally relevant. The audience gives in under the pressure of volume.

**Example.** Mountains of charts and tables that look impressive but cannot be checked

**Diagnostic question.** Is all this information relevant? Can the audience evaluate it?

**Psychological source.** Authority through volume

**Related fallacies:** [[Lying with Statistics]], [[Plain Truth Fallacy|Plain Truth Fallacy / KISS Principle]]
