**2.4.1.1** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.4 Domain 4 Comparison|2.4 Comparison]] · subtype: False Equation

**Formulation.** An improper comparison of two things in order to reach a false conclusion

**Mechanism of the violation.** Comparison by an irrelevant feature: two objects are alike in one respect, but the conclusion is drawn about another. Essential differences are ignored.

**Example.** “Just as a street cat needs to roam, a normal adult cannot be tied to one partner”

**Diagnostic question.** In what exactly are the compared things alike? Is this similarity relevant to the conclusion?

**Psychological source.** Thinking by analogy without checking

**Related fallacies:** 2.3.2, 2.4.2
