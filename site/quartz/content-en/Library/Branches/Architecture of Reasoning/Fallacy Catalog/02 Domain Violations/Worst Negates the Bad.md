**2.4.3.3** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.4 Domain 4 Comparison|2.4 Comparison]] · subtype: Unstable Criteria

**Formulation.** A bad situation is “not so bad” because it could have been worse

**Mechanism of the violation.** The criterion shifts: comparison with something worse does not make the bad good.

**Example.** “I cried because I had no shoes until I saw a man who had no feet”; “$7.25? In India it’s less!”

**Diagnostic question.** Does the existence of something worse make the situation acceptable?

**Psychological source.** Relative deprivation, gaslighting

**Related fallacies:** [[Moving the Goalposts]], 2.4.1
