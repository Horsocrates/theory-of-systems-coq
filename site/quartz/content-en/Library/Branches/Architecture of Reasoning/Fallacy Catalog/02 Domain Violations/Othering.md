**2.1.2.9** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.1 Domain 1 Recognition|2.1 Recognition]] · subtype: Object Substitution (A→B)

**Formulation.** Facts and arguments are rejected because their source is “not like us”

**Mechanism of the violation.** Object substitution: instead of the arguments, membership in an “alien” group is evaluated. The extreme form is dehumanization.

**Example.** “Interns don’t need rest—they’re young, they’re used to it”; “with them everything is different—you can’t treat them the way you treat us”

**Diagnostic question.** Am I considering the arguments, or rejecting them because the source is “alien”?

**Psychological source.** Xenophobia, in-group bias

**Related fallacies:** [[Identity Fallacy]], [[Name Calling|Name-Calling]], Dehumanization
