**2.6.4.10** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.6 Domain 6 Reflection|2.6 Reflection]] · subtype: Immunization from Testing

**Formulation.** Refusing to take part in politics because “everyone” is corrupt

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reflection is blocked by cynicism: a universal accusation justifies inaction. Circular logic.

**Example.** “All taxi drivers cheat—why ask the price?”; “All doctors are in cahoots—why go?”

**Diagnostic question.** Does the imperfection of a system justify a complete refusal?

**Psychological source.** Cynicism, learned helplessness

**Related fallacies:** 3.2, [[Deliberate Ignorance]]
