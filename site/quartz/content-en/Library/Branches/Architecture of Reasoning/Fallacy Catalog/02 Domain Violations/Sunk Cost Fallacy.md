**2.6.2.4** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.6 Domain 6 Reflection|2.6 Reflection]] · subtype: Inability to Revise

**Formulation.** Justifying further investment on the grounds that what has already been invested would otherwise be lost

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reflection is distorted: past losses should not affect the evaluation of future decisions.

**Example.** “I can’t stop playing—I’ve already lost the rent money, I have to win it back!”

**Diagnostic question.** Is continuing justified in itself? Or only because of what has been invested?

**Psychological source.** Loss aversion, escalation of commitment

**Related fallacies:** 2.6.2, 2.6.1
