**2.5.3.2** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.5 Domain 5 Inference|2.5 Inference]] · subtype: Chain Error

**Formulation.** “One thing inevitably leads to another”—a chain whose links are not justified

**Mechanism of the violation.** The chain is not justified: each transition requires proof but is passed off as inevitable.

**Example.** “Allow the phone at dinner → he’ll neglect his studies → he’ll end up without a job”—each link passed off as inevitable

**Diagnostic question.** Is each link in the chain justified?

**Psychological source.** Catastrophizing, fear

**Related fallacies:** [[Excluded Middle Fallacy]], 2.5.2
