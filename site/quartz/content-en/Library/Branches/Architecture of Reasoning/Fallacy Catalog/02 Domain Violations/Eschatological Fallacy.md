**2.3.1.1** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.3 Domain 3 Framework Selection|2.3 Framework Selection]] · subtype: Category Mismatch

**Formulation.** Arguing “This world will soon end, therefore...”

**Mechanism of the violation.** The framework of “the end of the world” is applied to ordinary decisions, removing the ordinary criteria of evaluation. Category mismatch: eschatology as a ground for practical conclusions.

**Example.** “Since the world is ending soon, you won’t need your savings anyway—give them to me”

**Diagnostic question.** Is “the end of the world” a relevant ground for this decision?

**Psychological source.** Apocalyptic thinking

**Related fallacies:** 2.3.5, [[Appeal to Heaven]]
