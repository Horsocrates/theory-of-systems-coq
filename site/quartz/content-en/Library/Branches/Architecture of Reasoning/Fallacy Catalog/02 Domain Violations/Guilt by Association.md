**2.1.2.4** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.1 Domain 1 Recognition|2.1 Recognition]] · subtype: Object Substitution (A→B)

**Formulation.** Refuting a position through the negative ethos of the group to which the speaker belongs

**Mechanism of the violation.** Object substitution: instead of the argument (A), the speaker’s group membership (B) is evaluated. Partisanship does not determine truth-value.

**Example.** “Don’t listen to him—he’s from that outfit”; “You were friends with that man?”

**Diagnostic question.** Am I evaluating the argument or the group to which the speaker belongs?

**Psychological source.** Tribalism, stereotypes

**Related fallacies:** [[Ad Hominem Argument]], [[Argument from Motives]]
