**2.1.3.2** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.1 Domain 1 Recognition|2.1 Recognition]] · subtype: Data Filtering

**Formulation.** Automatically ignoring relevant data because they come from outside one’s own discipline

**Mechanism of the violation.** Filtering at the input: data are sifted out not by relevance but by source. “This isn’t from our field” is a criterion unrelated to truth-value.

**Example.** “This may be relevant, but it’s not at all what we’re working on in our field right now”

**Diagnostic question.** Am I rejecting the information because of its source or because of its content?

**Psychological source.** Professional identity, in-group bias

**Related fallacies:** [[Availability Bias]], [[Blood Is Thicker Than Water]]
