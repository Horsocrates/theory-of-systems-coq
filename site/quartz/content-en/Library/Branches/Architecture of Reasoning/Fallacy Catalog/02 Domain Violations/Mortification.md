**2.3.5.8** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.3 Domain 3 Framework Selection|2.3 Framework Selection]] · subtype: Irrelevant Criterion

**Formulation.** An attempt to “subdue the flesh” through extreme exercise, fasting, or pain

**Mechanism of the violation.** The framework “suffering = good/achievement.” Pain, which is a warning, is interpreted as a goal. Intensity of suffering ≠ value of the result.

**Example.** Extreme “insanity” workouts not for health but to “punish the body”; self-flagellation

**Diagnostic question.** Is pain a goal or a signal? Does this serve a real goal?

**Psychological source.** Asceticism, endorphin dependence

**Related fallacies:** [[E for Effort|‘E’ for Effort]], Hedonism
