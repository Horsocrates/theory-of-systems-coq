**2.3.5.9** · [[02 Domain Violations|Domain Violations (Type 2)]] → [[2.3 Domain 3 Framework Selection|2.3 Framework Selection]] · subtype: Irrelevant Criterion

**Formulation.** All who wore the uniform and carried out orders deserve honor—regardless of the cause

**Mechanism of the violation.** Irrelevant criterion: “carried out orders” ≠ “deserves honor.” Refuted at Nuremberg.

**Example.** Honoring those who carried out orders on any side “for their service”—without asking what cause they served; “he was just doing his job”

**Diagnostic question.** What exactly was this soldier fighting for?

**Psychological source.** Deference to authority, tribal loyalty

**Related fallacies:** [[Finish the Job]], [[Heroes All]], 1.B.12
