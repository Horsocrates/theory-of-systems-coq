**3.3.1** · [[03 Sequence Violations|Violations of Sequence (Type 3)]] → [[3.3 Burden Inversion|3.3 Inversion]] · subtype: Burden Inversion

**Formulation.** Demanding that the opponent refute a claim instead of proving it oneself

**Mechanism of the violation.** A violation of sequence: the one who asserts must prove. “Prove that it isn’t so” turns logic upside down.

**Example.** “Aliens are among us! Prove they aren’t! You can’t? Then I’m right!”

**Diagnostic question.** Who must prove—the one who asserts or the one who doubts?

**Psychological source.** Rhetorical trick, evasion

**Related fallacies:** [[Argument from Ignorance]], 3.2
