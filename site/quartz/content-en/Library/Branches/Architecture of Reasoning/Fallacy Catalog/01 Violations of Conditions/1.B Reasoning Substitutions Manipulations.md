**Group 1.B** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]]

In place of an argument, an influence is presented. Mimicry of reasoning.

**Principle:** Reasoning is replaced by pressure or deception.

Fallacies in the group: **33**, by mechanism of violation:

## Threat Instead of Argument

**Mechanism 1.B.1** · fallacies: 8

| ID | Fallacy | Formulation |
|---|---|---|
| 1.B.1.1 | [[Argumentum ad Baculum]] | “Persuasion” by force, violence, threats, or superior power |
| 1.B.1.2 | [[Just Do It]] | Imperious dismissal of subordinates’ moral objections, with an order to reach the goal by any means |
| 1.B.1.3 | [[No Discussion\|No Discussion / No Negotiation]] | Refusal of dialogue; the only options are unconditional surrender or annihilation |
| 1.B.1.4 | [[Plausible Deniability]] | Compelling subordinates to commit an act and take the responsibility for it, so as to shield the superior |
| 1.B.1.5 | [[Silent Treatment\|The Pout / Silent Treatment]] | Arbitrary refusal of dialogue or withdrawal from it before it is finished |
| 1.B.1.6 | [[Standard Version Fallacy]] | Declaring a translation “authorized,” which cuts off discussion of the original |
| 1.B.1.7 | [[Thousand Flowers Fallacy]] | Encouraging discussion in order to identify and punish dissenters |
| 1.B.1.8 | [[There Is No Alternative\|TINA / There Is No Alternative]] | Suppressing critical thinking by declaring that there is no alternative |

**Mechanism 1.B.2** · fallacies: 3

|---|---|---|

| 1.B.2.3 | [[Save the Children Fallacy]] | Winning support by showing the suffering of children on “our” side while ignoring the others |
