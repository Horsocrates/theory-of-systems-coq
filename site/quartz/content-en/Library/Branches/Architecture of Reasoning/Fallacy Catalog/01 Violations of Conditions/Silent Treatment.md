**1.B.1.5** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Threat Instead of Argument

**Formulation.** Arbitrary refusal of dialogue or withdrawal from it before it is finished

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reasoning is broken off unilaterally: the refusal to continue serves as a form of pressure. A passive-aggressive tactic.

**Example.** Boycotts, strikes as pressure; a neighbor who has not said hello for years after a dispute over a fence

**Diagnostic question.** Was the dialogue broken off with reasons given, or as a form of pressure?

**Psychological source.** Passive aggression, infantile response

**Related fallacies:** [[No Discussion|No Discussion / No Negotiation]], [[Non Recognition|Non-Recognition]]
