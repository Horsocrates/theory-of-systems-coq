**1.B.2.1** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Pity Instead of Argument

**Formulation.** A call to “root for the underdog” regardless of the merits of the question

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reasoning is replaced by an emotional response. In place of an analysis of the situation on its merits—an appeal to pity and to sympathy for the “underdog.” Emotion takes the place of argument.

**Example.** “These poor sweet little mice squeak, and the wicked cats eat them!”—as an argument against cats that ignores ecology

**Diagnostic question.** Is my sympathy for one side a ground for evaluating the situation on its merits?

**Psychological source.** Empathy, identification with the weak

**Related fallacies:** 1.B.3, 2.3.5
