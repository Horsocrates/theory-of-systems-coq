**1.A.3.1** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.A Question Framing Errors|1.A Questions]] · subtype: Several Questions Merged into One

**Formulation.** Several different questions are merged into one and demand a single answer

**Mechanism of the violation.** The question glues together several independent questions; a single “yes/no” answer forces one to answer all of them at once, although the answers may diverge.

**Example.** “Did you like our service and our prices—yes or no?”; “Are you for freedom and against responsibility?”

**Diagnostic question.** Are different questions glued together here? What would the answer to each be separately?

**Psychological source.** Impatience: a single answer instead of an analysis of the grounds

**Related fallacies:** [[Loaded Question]], 2.2.3
