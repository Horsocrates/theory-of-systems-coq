**1.B.3.7** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Emotion Instead of Argument

**Formulation.** The necessity of acting immediately, even if the action is ineffective

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reasoning is replaced by urgency: “people are frightened,” without the questions “what?” and “why?”

**Example.** Banning sandwiches on airplanes; inspecting toddlers’ shoes; Security Theater

**Diagnostic question.** Is the action effective? Or is it a mere show?

**Psychological source.** Action bias, anxiety

**Related fallacies:** [[Scare Tactics|Scare Tactics / Appeal to Fear]], [[Big but Fallacy|Big ‘But’ Fallacy]]
