**1.B.9** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Unverifiable Authority Instead of Argument

**Formulation.** The claim to know the will of God (or of History, of Fate), who approves of your position, so that no further justification is required

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reasoning is replaced by a reference to an unverifiable authority. “God commanded it” makes both checking and objection impossible. A renunciation of reasoning through an appeal to the absolute.

**Example.** “So it pleases Heaven—discussion is superfluous”; “The higher will is on our side—there is nothing to argue about”

**Diagnostic question.** How did you learn the will of God? Is it possible that you are mistaken? Is the position open to checking?

**Psychological source.** The desire for absolute certainty, avoidance of responsibility

**Related fallacies:** 2.6.4
