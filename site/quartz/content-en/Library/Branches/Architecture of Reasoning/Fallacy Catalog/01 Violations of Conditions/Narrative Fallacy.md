**1.B.2.2** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Emotion Instead of Argument

**Formulation.** Persuasion through a “touching” or horrifying story instead of logical arguments

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reasoning is replaced by emotional influence: a story persuades more strongly than logic, but it is not proof. Narratives are irrefutable, even when they are invented.

**Example.** Dickens’s *A Christmas Carol*; using a “poster child” to promote a position

**Diagnostic question.** Does the story prove a general principle? Or does it only arouse emotions?

**Psychological source.** Narrative bias, empathy

**Related fallacies:** 1.B.2, 1.B.3
