**1.B.11.2** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Quasi-Hypnotic Influence / Unverifiable Authority

**Formulation.** A claim to unverifiable “secret knowledge” as a way to avoid criticism or to create a hierarchy

**Mechanism of the violation.** A false appeal to esoteric knowledge. It is not to be confused with genuine esoteric/tacit knowledge, which really exists. The fallacy lies in using “inexpressibility” as a shield against criticism instead of passing knowledge on.

**Example.** “My theory is correct, but you won’t understand—it takes years of initiation”; “This is sacred knowledge, inaccessible to the uninitiated”; “You can’t judge, because you haven’t been through it”

**Diagnostic question.** Does this “knowledge” have verifiable results? Or is unverifiability being used as a defense against criticism?

**Psychological source.** The desire to belong to the chosen few

**Related fallacies:** [[Argumentum ad Mysteriam]], [[Appeal to Heaven]]
