**1.B.1.1** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Threat Instead of Argument

**Formulation.** “Persuasion” by force, violence, threats, or superior power

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reasoning is entirely replaced by coercion. Force does not prove one right. “Agree or you will suffer” is a threat, not an argument.

**Example.** “Hand over your wallet or you’ll get hit over the head”; “Either you sign or you’re fired”

**Diagnostic question.** Am I being persuaded or coerced? Does being right follow from force?

**Psychological source.** The will to power, intolerance

**Related fallacies:** 1.B.10
