**1.A.4** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.A Question Framing Errors|1.A Errors of Posing]] · subtype: Exclusion of Topics

**Formulation.** Unilateral declaration of topics or arguments as “sacred” and not subject to discussion

**Mechanism of the violation.** The conditions are violated: part of the space of arguments is closed a priori. Reasoning cannot begin honestly.

**Example.** “We don’t discuss my drinking”; “this subject isn’t raised in our family”

**Diagnostic question.** Is the exclusion of the topic justified? Or is it an evasion of uncomfortable questions?

**Psychological source.** Defensiveness, sacred values

**Related fallacies:** [[Appeal to Privacy|MYOB / Appeal to Privacy]], [[A Priori Argument]], [[Calling Cards|Calling ‘Cards’]]
