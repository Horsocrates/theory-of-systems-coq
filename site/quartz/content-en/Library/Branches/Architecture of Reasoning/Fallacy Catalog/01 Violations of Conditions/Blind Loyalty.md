**1.B.12.2** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Abandoning Reasoning through Delegation

**Formulation.** An argument or action is right simply because a respected leader/source said so

**Mechanism of the violation.** One’s own reasoning and conscience are subordinated to authority. “I was just following orders” is a renunciation of moral responsibility, not a justification.

**Example.** “That’s what I was told to do”; “I was just following orders”

**Diagnostic question.** Is an order/authority a sufficient ground? Does obedience relieve me of my responsibility?

**Psychological source.** Obedience to authority (the Milgram experiment)

**Related fallacies:** [[Big Brain Little Brain Fallacy|Big Brain/Little Brain Fallacy]], [[Appeal to Heaven]]
