**1.B.7** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Creating a False Ethos through Jargon

**Formulation.** Abuse of abbreviations and professional jargon to create the impression of being an “insider” or to confuse

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reasoning is replaced by a display of belonging to a group. In place of an argument—a signal “I am one of you” or a barrier to understanding. The content is hidden behind the form.

**Example.** “K-12 with ASD are often both GT and LD”—intelligible only to insiders

**Diagnostic question.** Does the jargon serve clarification or obfuscation? Can the same thing be said in plain language?

**Psychological source.** The desire to belong, the desire for power through knowledge
