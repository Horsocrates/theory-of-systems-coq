**1.B.15** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Tactical Manipulation

**Formulation.** At the decisive moment an important fact is suddenly “remembered,” and after the damage is done it is declared immaterial

**Mechanism of the violation.** Manipulation of the process: information is presented at a moment when the opponent cannot respond. Not permitted in court.

**Example.** “Oops, I forgot: you’ve already been tried for this twice, haven’t you?!”—at the end of a trial; a sudden “I just remembered” at the end of negotiations

**Diagnostic question.** Why has this information appeared precisely now? Is there an opportunity to respond?

**Psychological source.** Tactical thinking, manipulation

**Related fallacies:** [[Mala Fides|Mala Fides / Arguing in Bad Faith]], [[Alternative Truth]]
