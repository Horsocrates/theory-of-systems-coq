**1.B.1.6** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Threat Instead of Argument

**Formulation.** Declaring a translation “authorized,” which cuts off discussion of the original

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reasoning is cut off by decree: the “official version” closes the discussion of meaning.

**Example.** The Vulgate; the King James Version as the “only correct” one; approved translations

**Diagnostic question.** Why is precisely this version the “right” one?

**Psychological source.** Authority, convenience

**Related fallacies:** 1.B.11, [[Othering]]
