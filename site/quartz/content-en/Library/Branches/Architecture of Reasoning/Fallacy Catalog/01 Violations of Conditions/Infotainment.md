**1.B.8.3** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Denial of the Conditions of Reasoning / Disinformation

**Formulation.** Deliberate mixing of facts, news, lies, and entertainment for ideological and commercial ends

**Mechanism of the violation.** The destruction of the conditions of reasoning: the boundary between fact and fiction is blurred deliberately. The audience cannot tell news from entertainment.

**Example.** The tabloid press; mixing news and entertainment with the disclaimer “for entertainment purposes only”

**Diagnostic question.** Is this news or entertainment? Have the facts been checked?

**Psychological source.** The desire to be entertained, a lack of critical sense

**Related fallacies:** [[Big Lie Technique]], [[Dog Whistle Politics|Dog-Whistle Politics]]
