**1.B.11.1** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Quasi-Hypnotic Influence Instead of Argument

**Formulation.** Use of rituals, mysteries, incomprehensible language, and atmosphere to “persuade” without arguments

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reasoning is replaced by an influence on perception: a darkened room, incense, chanting, rituals, and incomprehensible language create a quasi-hypnotic effect. What persuades is the atmosphere, not logic.

**Example.** Reading sacred texts in an incomprehensible ancient language; using Latin terms to lend weight

**Diagnostic question.** Am I persuaded by the content or by the form of presentation? Do I understand what exactly is being asserted?

**Psychological source.** Susceptibility to ritual, reverence for the incomprehensible
