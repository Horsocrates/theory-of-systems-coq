**1.B.1.4** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Threat Instead of Argument

**Formulation.** Compelling subordinates to commit an act and take the responsibility for it, so as to shield the superior

**Mechanism of the violation.** Manipulation through power: reasoning is replaced by an order with concealment built in. Responsibility is shifted.

**Example.** “Arrange an accident for him, but I don’t want to know anything about it!”

**Diagnostic question.** Who actually made the decision? Who bears the moral responsibility?

**Psychological source.** Power, moral disengagement

**Related fallacies:** [[Just Do It]], [[Passive Voice Fallacy]]
