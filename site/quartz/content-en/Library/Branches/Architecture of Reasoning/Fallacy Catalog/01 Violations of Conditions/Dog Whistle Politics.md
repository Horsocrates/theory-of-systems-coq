**1.B.3.1** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Emotion Instead of Argument

**Formulation.** Use of code words/phrases that trigger a reflexive emotional reaction instead of reasoning

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reasoning is replaced by a trigger: a short phrase provokes an immediate emotional reaction, bypassing analysis. An attempt to deconstruct the trigger meets with fury or bewilderment.

**Example.** A speaker “whistles” signal words to the audience instead of examining the arguments

**Diagnostic question.** Am I reacting to the word/phrase reflexively, or analyzing the content?

**Psychological source.** Tribalism, emotional contagion

**Related fallacies:** 1.B.3, Reductionism
