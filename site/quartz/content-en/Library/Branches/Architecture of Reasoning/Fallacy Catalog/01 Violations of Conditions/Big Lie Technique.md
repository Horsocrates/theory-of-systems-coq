**1.B.8.1** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Denial of the Conditions of Reasoning / Disinformation

**Formulation.** Repeating a lie in different forms until it becomes part of the discourse

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reasoning is replaced by repetition. A lie does not become truth through repetition, but it begins to be perceived as an “already known fact.” The more brazen the lie, the more convincing it is.

**Example.** The “Jewish question” of Nazi propaganda—there was no “question” at all; a lie too big to be suspected is repeated until it becomes familiar.

**Diagnostic question.** Do I accept this as fact because I have heard it many times, or because there is evidence?

**Psychological source.** Illusory truth effect

**Related fallacies:** [[Alternative Truth]], [[Bandwagon Fallacy]], 2.1.1
