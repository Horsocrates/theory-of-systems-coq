**1.B.3.6** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Emotion Instead of Argument

**Formulation.** Making important decisions in the grip of strong emotions—decisions that are later regretted

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reasoning is replaced by an emotional state: decisions made “in the heat of the moment.”

**Example.** After a theft in the stairwell, the building bought the most expensive security without comparing anything—a purchase made “on an empty stomach”

**Diagnostic question.** Would we have made this decision in a calm state?

**Psychological source.** Hot cognition, emotional decision-making

**Related fallacies:** [[Scare Tactics|Scare Tactics / Appeal to Fear]], [[Big but Fallacy|Big ‘But’ Fallacy]]
