**1.B.1.2** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Threat Instead of Argument

**Formulation.** Imperious dismissal of subordinates’ moral objections, with an order to reach the goal by any means

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reasoning is replaced by an order: moral objections are swept aside by the force of authority. The use of unethical methods is implied.

**Example.** “You found a cemetery on the site? Find a way! Make it disappear! It’s a contract worth a million—we need it by Tuesday”

**Diagnostic question.** Are my moral objections being answered, or simply brushed aside?

**Psychological source.** Power, impatience

**Related fallacies:** [[Argumentum ad Baculum]], [[Plausible Deniability]], 1.B.12
