**1.B.3.2** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Emotion Instead of Argument

**Formulation.** An attempt to strengthen an argument with obscene or sexualized language unrelated to its content

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reasoning is replaced by emotional impact: shock, indignation, or identification through language. Obscenity does not make an argument stronger.

**Example.** Adding “adult” scenes to a film only for the sake of ratings; smut instead of an argument

**Diagnostic question.** Does this language strengthen the argument, or does it only produce an emotional reaction?

**Psychological source.** The desire to shock, identification with a group

**Related fallacies:** 1.B.3, [[Red Herring]]
