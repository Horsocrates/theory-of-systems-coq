**1.B.1.8** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Threat Instead of Argument

**Formulation.** Suppressing critical thinking by declaring that there is no alternative

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reasoning is cut off by authority: alternatives are declared nonexistent. Fait accompli.

**Example.** “If you like it, stay; if you don’t, leave”; “Accept it”; “It is what it is”

**Diagnostic question.** Are there really no alternatives? Or have they been declared nonexistent?

**Psychological source.** Power play, intellectual laziness

**Related fallacies:** [[Taboo|Taboo / Dogmatism]], [[Finish the Job]]
