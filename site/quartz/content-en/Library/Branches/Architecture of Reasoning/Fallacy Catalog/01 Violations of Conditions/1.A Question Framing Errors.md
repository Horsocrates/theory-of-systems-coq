**Group 1.A** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]]

Dishonest questions that distort the conditions of reasoning before it begins.

**Principle:** An honest question is directed toward absolute truth and understanding.

<!-- PROSE:BEGIN -->
## The Ethics of the Group: Volitional Distortions

To ask a question is to make a choice, and a choice has an intention. The five defects of the group are one mechanism: **the choice of a question with an intention other than truth**; the corruption of the parts of the act (goal · direction · ground) is only a trace of a distorted intention. The same question asked out of not-knowing is an entrance to clarification, not a corruption. Some distortions are directed against the other (the loaded question imputes, bypassing the will of the other; merged questions force one choice where there are two; an imposed taboo is power over another’s field of questioning), others against oneself (a self-imposed taboo, evasion by context, the alibi of the impossible); all are against truth. Hence the principle of the group becomes a conclusion: honest questioning is movement along the root, that is, along logic, and therefore it is right; sophistry is the hiding of logic behind formulations: turned against the will of another for one’s own ends, it is evil at the entrance of reasoning; turned toward oneself, it is harm. The anatomy of the act and the analysis of all five are on the page [[Question]]; the proof is in [[Formalization]].
