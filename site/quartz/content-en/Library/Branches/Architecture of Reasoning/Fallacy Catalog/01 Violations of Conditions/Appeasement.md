**1.B.10.1** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Pressure Instead of Argument

**Formulation.** Yielding to demands not because they are justified but because the one demanding causes trouble

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reasoning about who is right is replaced by a calculation of costs. “It is cheaper to give in than to argue” is no argument for the rightness of the demand. It encourages manipulative behavior.

**Example.** “The customer is always right, even when he is wrong. Give him what he wants so he’ll leave us alone—it’s cheaper than going to court”

**Diagnostic question.** Am I yielding because the demand is just, or because it is easier?

**Psychological source.** Conflict avoidance, short-term optimization

**Related fallacies:** 1.B.1
