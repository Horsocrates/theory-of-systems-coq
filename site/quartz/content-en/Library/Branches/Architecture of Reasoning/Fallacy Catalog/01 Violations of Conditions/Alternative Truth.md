**1.B.8** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Denial of the Conditions of Reasoning

**Formulation.** Denial of the distinction between fact and fiction, between truth and lies, as such

**Mechanism of the violation.** The very condition of reasoning is destroyed—the distinction between truth and lies. If this distinction does not exist, reasoning is impossible in principle. An attack on the foundation.

**Example.** Insisting on one’s own version in spite of documented reality

**Diagnostic question.** Does the interlocutor admit the possibility of being wrong? Do they recognize the distinction between fact and opinion?

**Psychological source.** The desire to control the narrative

**Related fallacies:** 4.2
