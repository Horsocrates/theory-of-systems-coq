**1.B.3.5** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Emotion Instead of Argument

**Formulation.** Using a crisis and fear to impose a decision that would be unacceptable on calm consideration

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reasoning is replaced by fear: panic is used to push through unacceptable decisions.

**Example.** “If you don’t do as I say—all is lost!”; an alarm salesman opens the conversation with a rundown of burglaries

**Diagnostic question.** Would this decision be made on calm consideration?

**Psychological source.** Fear response, crisis exploitation

**Related fallacies:** [[Playing on Emotion|Playing on Emotion / Sob Story]], Shock Doctrine
