**1.B.3.4** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Emotion Instead of Argument

**Formulation.** Public reading of a list of names for emotional effect

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reasoning is replaced by pathos: names produce a mystical effect, but this is not an argument.

**Example.** Reading out a long list of signatures “against” instead of an argument—the weight of numbers instead of analysis

**Diagnostic question.** Does the listing of names prove anything? Or does it only create an effect?

**Psychological source.** Emotional impact of names

**Related fallacies:** 1.B.2, [[Narrative Fallacy]]
