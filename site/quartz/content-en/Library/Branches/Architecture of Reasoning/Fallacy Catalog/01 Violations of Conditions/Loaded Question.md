**1.A.6** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.A Question Framing Errors|1.A Questions]] · subtype: A Question with a Built-In Assumption

**Formulation.** Demand for a direct answer to a question with a built-in assumption

**Mechanism of the violation.** The assumption is built into the question itself: any direct answer—both “yes” and “no”—accepts it.

**Example.** “Just answer yes or no: did you think you could get away with plagiarism without consequences?”; “Why did you rob the bank?”

**Diagnostic question.** Does the question contain hidden assumptions? Can it be answered without accepting them?

**Psychological source.** The desire to back the other into a corner

**Related fallacies:** [[Complex Question]], 2.2.3
