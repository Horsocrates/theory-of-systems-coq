**1.B.1.7** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Threat Instead of Argument

**Formulation.** Encouraging discussion in order to identify and punish dissenters

**Mechanism of the violation.** Manipulation: “free discussion” is a trap for identifying dissenters.

**Example.** Mao’s “Let a hundred flowers bloom” campaign—the exposure and elimination of dissidents

**Diagnostic question.** Is it safe to voice disagreement?

**Psychological source.** Entrapment, authoritarian control

**Related fallacies:** 1.B.1, [[Mala Fides|Mala Fides / Arguing in Bad Faith]]
