**1.B.2.3** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Pity Instead of Argument

**Formulation.** Winning support by showing the suffering of children on “our” side while ignoring the others

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reasoning is replaced by selective pathos: only the “right” victims are shown, to justify an intervention.

**Example.** “Let’s save the children on our block!”—and silence about the children on the next block; pity by the list

**Diagnostic question.** Are the victims of all sides shown? Or only the “useful” ones?

**Psychological source.** Selective empathy, propaganda

**Related fallacies:** 1.B.2, 2.1.3
