**1.B.13.1** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Gain Instead of Argument

**Formulation.** “Persuasion” by bribes, gifts, or favors

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reasoning is replaced by a material incentive. The reverse side of ad baculum: a reward instead of a threat. Bribery does not make a position true.

**Example.** Offering money or favors in exchange for “agreement” with a position

**Diagnostic question.** Do I agree because I am convinced, or because it is profitable?

**Psychological source.** Material interest

**Related fallacies:** 1.B.1, 1.B.10
