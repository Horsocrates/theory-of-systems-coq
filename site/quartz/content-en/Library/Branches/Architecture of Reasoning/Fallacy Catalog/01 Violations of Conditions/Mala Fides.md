**1.B.14** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Deception Instead of Argument

**Formulation.** Using an argument that the one making it knows to be invalid

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reasoning is replaced by conscious deception: the speaker knows the argument is false. It includes “motivational truth”—a lie for a “good cause.”

**Example.** A lawyer defends a client they know to be guilty; a debater upholds a position they do not themselves believe, for the sake of winning

**Diagnostic question.** Does the speaker believe their own arguments?

**Psychological source.** Machiavellianism, the end justifies the means

**Related fallacies:** [[Alternative Truth]], Self Deception
