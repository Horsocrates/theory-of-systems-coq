**1.B.8.4** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Disinformation

**Formulation.** Limiting what is said to prepared phrases for maximum impact

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reasoning is replaced by the repetition of scripts: prepared lines are broadcast for effect, in place of a dialogue.

**Example.** A press secretary repeats the same phrases regardless of the question; prepared lines instead of an answer

**Diagnostic question.** Is the person answering the question or repeating a prepared line?

**Psychological source.** Message control, propaganda

**Related fallacies:** [[Big Lie Technique]], Dog Whistle
