**1.B.8.2** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Denial of the Conditions of Reasoning / Disinformation

**Formulation.** Denying or distorting known facts to make the victim doubt their own sanity

**Mechanism of the violation.** An attack on the very possibility of reasoning: trust in one’s own perception and memory is undermined. “Who are you going to believe—me or your own eyes?”

**Example.** “You saw the bill with your own eyes? You imagined it—you’re always mixing things up and making things up”

**Diagnostic question.** Am I being made to doubt what I saw/remember? Who gains from my doubt?

**Psychological source.** Manipulation, control

**Related fallacies:** [[Big Lie Technique]], 1.B.12
