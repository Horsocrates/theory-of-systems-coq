**1.A.5** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.A Question Framing Errors|1.A Errors of Posing]] · subtype: Exclusion by Context

**Formulation.** Rejection of a valid argument because of the “wrong” place or forum

**Mechanism of the violation.** The conditions are violated arbitrarily: the argument is not considered because of its context.

**Example.** “The work chat is no place for this question”; “This is not the proper forum”—when you are losing

**Diagnostic question.** Does the place make the argument invalid?

**Psychological source.** Deflection, context manipulation

**Related fallacies:** [[Disciplinary Blinders]], [[Taboo|Taboo / Dogmatism]]
