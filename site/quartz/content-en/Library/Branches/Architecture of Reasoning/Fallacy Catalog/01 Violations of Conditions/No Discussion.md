**1.B.1.3** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Threat Instead of Argument

**Formulation.** Refusal of dialogue; the only options are unconditional surrender or annihilation

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reasoning is rejected in principle. The opponent is portrayed as a monster devoid of reason. Force replaces argument.

**Example.** “There is nothing to discuss—do as you are told”; “Whoever argues is against us”

**Diagnostic question.** Why is dialogue impossible? Is the opponent really devoid of reason?

**Psychological source.** Machismo, dehumanization

**Related fallacies:** [[Argumentum ad Baculum]], [[Moral Superiority]]
