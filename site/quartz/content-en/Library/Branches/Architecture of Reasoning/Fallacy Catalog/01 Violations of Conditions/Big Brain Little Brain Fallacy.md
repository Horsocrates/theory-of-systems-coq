**1.B.12.1** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Abandoning Reasoning through Delegation

**Formulation.** The leader says: “Don’t think with your little brain—think with my big one. I take all the responsibility on myself”

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reasoning is delegated entirely to another. A person gives up their own judgment and responsibility. It is a refusal to reason at all, not an error within reasoning.

**Example.** “You need not worry about whether your actions are right—I, the Leader, take all the responsibility on myself. Just carry out orders”

**Diagnostic question.** Am I giving up my own judgment? Does someone’s order relieve me of my responsibility?

**Psychological source.** The desire to shed responsibility, obedience to authority

**Related fallacies:** [[Blind Loyalty]], [[Appeal to Heaven]]
