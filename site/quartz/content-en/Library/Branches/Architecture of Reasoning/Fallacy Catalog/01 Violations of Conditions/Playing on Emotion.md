**1.B.3.3** · [[01 Violations of Conditions|Violations of Conditions (Type 1)]] → [[1.B Reasoning Substitutions Manipulations|1.B Manipulations]] · subtype: Emotion Instead of Argument

**Formulation.** A pure argument from emotion that ignores the facts—nothing but the arousal of feelings

**Mechanism of the violation.** Reasoning is replaced by pure pathos. The facts are ignored; there is only an emotional picture.

**Example.** “Picture the weeping mothers over the empty cribs! Let’s hang all the witches!”

**Diagnostic question.** Are there facts and arguments here? Or only emotions?

**Psychological source.** Empathy as an instrument of manipulation

**Related fallacies:** 1.B.2, [[Narrative Fallacy]], Apathetic Fallacy
