## Where the Chapter Stands

Four chapters have built the ascent. Chapter 1 gave the tower of role-types—strictly growing, generative, indexed by the ordinal-process $\omega$. Chapter 2 supplied it with a scale of depth: each storey corresponds to a rung of omniscience, and this gradation is constructive. Chapters 3 and 4 put the scale to work, rolling back two classical “walls”—determinacy and well-quasi-ordering—to processes in which nonconstructivity is localized at one point.

What remains is the question of synthesis: what makes these four results *one* hierarchy rather than a list of four? The answer is two threads that stitch the ascent into a single whole: a common ordinal-process index and one recurring rung of omniscience. This chapter draws them out and formulates the flagship of the part.

## The First Thread: A Single Ordinal-Process Index

The tower of role-types (Chapter 1) is indexed by the height $\omega$, given as the limit of a process. The ascent of determinacy (Chapter 3) is indexed by its rungs—and their height is *the same* $\omega=\mathrm{OLim}$, the limit of the same numbering process[^1]. This is not a coincidence of notation: both structures are generated by *one* rule—“above what is reached, one more stage”—and the limit is taken as a process, not as a completed supremum.

*The first thread: one index runs through the whole ascent—the ordinal-process $\omega$. The height of the hierarchy is not a number but a process; and it is the same process on each of its segments.*

Hence also the common feature of generativity: neither the tower nor the ascent of determinacy has a maximal storey—above anything reached there is a next one. The ascent is one already by its index.

## The Second Thread: One LPO Rung Recurs

The second thread is deeper and more unexpected. Storey 1 of the tower (Chapter 2) corresponded to the LPO rung—the question “fires or never.” The open rung of determinacy (Chapter 3)—the question “will player I ever win”—also turned out to be exactly LPO. This is *one and the same* rung: to decide the question of storey 1 and to decide open determinacy are *equivalent* abilities[^2].

And the same dose of omniscience—LPO—is the $\Sigma^0_1$-“edge” of the method of well-quasi-ordering over the decidable (Chapter 4): where the decidability of the continuations is lost, exactly this rung begins. The depth of the hierarchy, the ascent of determinacy, and the edge of the wqo method are *one ladder of rungs*, not three parallel ones.

*The second thread: one rung of omniscience—LPO—recurs throughout the ascent. These are not different “infinities” in different problems but one dose, entering at one and the same point.*

And, like every rung (Chapter 2), it is constructive: the Law of Excluded Middle collapses it everywhere in the same way. The unity of the rung is also a P4 fact: the classical view, for which everything is already completed, does not see that this is *the very same* dose.

## The Zero-Axiom Base and Localized Nonconstructivity

Under both threads lies a common ground: *the bottom of the ascent is free of the oracle*. Finite determinacy is computed by backward induction; the well-quasi-ordering of ℕ, by well-founded descent; equality on the zeroth storey is decidable. All of this is 0 axioms, pure processes of the Element side[^3].

Nonconstructivity, for its part, enters *not throughout* but at precisely named rungs—and this is always the one LPO rung, at the step from the finite to the infinite. A sharp picture results: *the whole lower part of the ascent is axiom-free, while completed infinity gathers into one localized dose*, recurring across the structure but nowhere spread out as a wall.

## The Registry of “Former Walls” Updated

Part X marked out the “high” constructions of set theory with a registry of “former walls” and left the minimal bad sequence and the Borel tower with the status “process form not invoked.” The present part *raises* this status: their lower rungs are built as processes, and both “walls” move to *“partially rolled back”*[^4].

{*Of the classical “high” constructions, the only object that P4 genuinely rules out is completed actual infinity itself. The rest is either reached as an operation-role or built as a process; the “walls” have turned out to be ascents.*}

This is the demonstration of the strength of the stance: where the familiar packaging sees a blank boundary that demands a completed object, P4 sees a graded ascent in which the reachable is reached and the unreachable is honestly named a horizon.

## The Hierarchy Is a Process

Now the flagship of the part can be stated directly.

*The hierarchy is a process.*

The role-limit side of the finitization boundary is neither a completed object (the cumulative, Borel, and projective towers as ready sets) nor a blank wall but an *upward ordinal-process ascent of role-types*: generative (without a top), graded by omniscience (depth is visible only under P4), with an axiom-free bottom and nonconstructivity localized in one rung.

This reframing stands in one row with those already traversed in the volume. A real number is a Cauchy process, not a completed object (Part IV). The algebraic closure $\overline{ℚ}$ is a generative tower without a maximal storey (Part XI). A manifold is a process of gluing charts (Part XII). The hierarchy is their set-theoretic sibling: *what classical mathematics thinks of as a completed tower-object is in fact a generative process*.

Let us also speak honestly about the level. The synthesis is the *placement and unification* of the proved stages by two threads, not new “high” theorems; full Kruskal and Borel determinacy remain horizons. The strength of the part lies not in a new summit being taken but in the summit turning out to be *unnecessary*: the content is reached by the ascent, and the completed summit-object was an artifact of the packaging.

## E/R/R Analysis: The Hierarchy as a System

Let us analyze by E/R/R not a separate stage now but *the whole ascent* as one system—in the generating order Rules $\to$ Roles $\to$ Elements.

**Rules (L5).** Two holding rules stitch the ascent together: the rule of indexing (“above what is reached, one more stage,” the limit taken as the process $\omega$) and the rule of the rung (the step from the finite to the infinite charges exactly LPO, and the Law of Excluded Middle collapses it in the same way). These two rules are the two threads of the synthesis.

**Roles (L4).** The ascent plays the role of a *process*, not of an object; its height has the role of an ordinal-process; the LPO rung, the role of a single measure of omniscience recurring across the structure. A “former wall” plays the role of a *horizon-ascent*, not of an exclusion.

**Elements (L1 + P4).** The bearers are the finitely reachable stages of all four constructions: the storeys of the tower, the finite truncations of games, the finite segments of descent. Under P4 these are actual; the completed summit-object is not actualized—it is precisely the only object ruled out.

**Well-formedness check.** The rules of indexing and of the rung are Rules; the ascent, the height, the rung, and the horizon are Roles; the finite stages are Elements; there are no overlaps. There is no self-application (P1): the rules stand above the ascent and are not its stages—the hierarchy does not contain itself as a storey. It is precisely this self-non-containment that separates a generative process from the paradoxical “set of all sets.”

**What the analysis yields.** It shows that “how large is the hierarchy” and “does it need completed infinity” are confusions of categories: they demand that the process-ascent be a completed Element-object. The correct questions are *how far one can climb* and *at which rung nonconstructivity enters*—and the part has answered both exactly.

With this the role-limit side receives its process form. The final synthesis of the volume (Part XX) will bring both sides of the finitization boundary together—and now it has something to bring together: not a flat wall but a graded process ascent, built here stage by stage.
