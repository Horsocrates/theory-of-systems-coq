## Where the Chapter Stands

Chapters 14.1–14.3 worked *within a single level*: the category as a metasystem (14.1), functors and the Yoneda embedding (14.2), the category of systems $\Sys(L)$ with the core of P3 (14.3). This chapter takes the step for whose sake the whole hierarchy was undertaken: it builds *functors between levels* and shows that their link is an exact categorical adjunction. This is the **crown of Part XIV**: the principle P2 (Complementarity) receives a machine form.

Two arrows connect adjacent levels. The *embedding*

$$
`embed`\ :\ \Sys(L)\ \longrightarrow\ \Sys(LS L)
$$

raises a system to the next level; the *forgetting*

$$
`forget`\ :\ \Sys(LS L)\ \rightharpoonup\ \Sys(L)
$$

lowers it back—but only *partially* (the symbol $\rightharpoonup$). The main result:

> **$`embed`\dashv`forget`$ is an adjunction, and this is P2 (Complementarity):**
> two complementary views of one structure, neither of which is complete by itself.

The anchors of the adjunction—`LevelFunctors.v`, `LevelAdjunction.v`, and (for the name P2) `ProcessAdjunction.v`—are all in the main branch (`origin/main`): they can be cited with confidence, as in 14.3. The general frame “equivalence of categories” rests on the new local layer `src/category/` (we mark it separately).

## The Hierarchy of Levels: The Relation $\ll$

The arrows `embed`/`forget` move along the *spine* of ToS—the strict order on levels. The type `Level` is generated by two constructors (the bottom `L1` and the passage to the next level, `LS`), and the relation $w\ll L$ (“the level $w$ is *below* $L$”) is a well-founded strict order.
[^1] We will need exactly four of its properties: transitivity ($w\ll L\ll LS L\Rightarrow w\ll LS L$), the fact $L\ll LS L$ (each level is below the next), *irreflexivity* $\neg (L\ll L)$—this is P1 itself (the exclusion of self-membership in the form “a level is not below itself”)—and the *decidability* of comparison. Irreflexivity will turn out to be exactly the place where `forget` runs into an obstruction.

## `embed`: A Level Embeds into the Next

The embedding of a system $S$ from level $L$ into $LS L$ keeps *the same* elements, *the same* criterion, and *the same* witness $w$; only the proof of the witness’s legitimacy changes: it was $w\ll L$ and becomes $w\ll LS L$, via transitivity and $L\ll LS L$. Nothing happens on the elements:

$$
`ElemOf` (LS L) (`embed`\ S)\ =\ `ElemOf` L S
$$

*definitionally* (`embed_obj_elem_eq`, `level_bridge`); a morphism is embedded by the same function on elements (`embed_preserves_structure`).

This is a *full-fledged functor*—`EmbedFunctor` $L:\Sys(L)\to\Sys(LS L)$: it preserves identity and composition, is *faithful* (`embed_faithful`), preserves embeddings, surjections, and isomorphisms (`embed_ preserves_ embedding`, `embed_ preserves_ surjection`, `embed_ preserves_ iso`), and takes the empty system to an initial one (`embed_ empty_ is_ initial`).
[^2] The substantive reading is direct: **the lower level sits in the upper one without distortion**—elements, criterion, and the network of morphisms are transferred one to one. The level-structure is “invisible from below”: from the standpoint of elements, $S$ and $`embed`\ S$ are indistinguishable.

## `forget`: The Partial Forgetting of a Level

The reverse move lowers a system from $LS L$ to $L$, and here an asymmetry arises. A system on $LS L$ is *forgettable* (`is_forgettable`) if its witness is strictly below $L$:

$$
`is_forgettable` L T\ :=\ (\text{the witness of } T)\ \ll\ L.
$$

Only under this hypothesis can one build `forget_obj` $L T H_f:\Sys(L)$: the legitimacy of the witness at the new level is given by the hypothesis $H_f$ itself.

**The P1 obstruction.**  Not every system on $LS L$ is forgettable. Take a system whose witness *equals* $L$ (`witness_L_system`): to forget it would mean obtaining $L\ll L$, which irreflexivity—P1—rules out. Therefore (`witness_L_not_forgettable`)

$$
`P1_obstructs_total_forget`\ :\ \exists T,\ \neg `is_forgettable` L T.
$$

Forgettability itself is *decidable* (`is_forgettable_dec`): for each system one can *compute* whether it can be lowered.

**Why `forget` does not package into a `Functor`.**  Since `forget` is defined only on part of the objects (the hypothesis $H_f$ is needed), it does *not* form an instance of the Rocq structure `Functor` (which requires a total `fobj` on all objects). This is an honest constitution, not laziness of formalization: **the adjunction is written as a set of standalone lemmas with explicit
forgettability hypotheses**, not as a pair of functors.
[^3] Nevertheless *all* the properties of the adjunction—the hom-bijection, naturality, unit, counit, triangles—are proved pointwise (the next section). `forget_mor` is faithful on its domain of definition and preserves embeddings (`forget_faithful`, `forget_ preserves_ embedding`).

## The Adjunction $`embed`\dashv`forget`$ Is P2

The heart of the chapter. Between levels there is a natural bijection of hom-sets:

$$
\Hom_{\Sys(LS L)}\!\big(`embed`\ S,\ T\big)\ \;\cong\;\
  \Hom_{\Sys(L)}\!\big(S,\ `forget`\ T\big),
$$

from left to right, `adj_forward`; from right to left, `adj_backward`; the two sides are mutually inverse (`level_adjunction`) and natural in $S$ and in $T$ (`adj_natural_S`, `adj_natural_T`, `adj_naturality_square`). By the definition of adjunction this means: **`embed` is the *left* adjoint, `forget` the *right* one.**
[^4]

**The unit and the counit.**  The unit $\eta_S:S\to`forget`(`embed` S)$ is an *isomorphism* (`adjunction_unit_is_iso`); moreover, $`forget`(`embed` S)=S$ holds *in the Leibniz sense* (`forget_embed_roundtrip`). The counit $\varepsilon_T:`embed`(`forget` T)\to T$ is an isomorphism for forgettable $T$ (`adjunction_ counit_ is_ iso_ for_ forgettable`). Both triangle identities hold (`triangle_identity_1`, `triangle_identity_2`). Since the unit is an isomorphism, the adjunction is “tight”; categorically this means that `embed` is *fully faithful*. *Faithfulness* is proved explicitly by machine (`embed_faithful`), and *fullness* follows from the unit being an isomorphism (`forget_ embed_ adjunction_ tight`); this agrees with what we already knew about `embed` directly.
[^5]

**Why this is P2 (Complementarity).**  P2 asserts that a system has *complementary* aspects that cannot be specified simultaneously. Categorically, this is exactly a pair of adjoints: `embed` *adds* the level-structure, `forget` *removes* it—two complementary views of one structure, neither of which by itself is the full picture. This thesis is assembled by machine: `P2_categorical`/`P2_holds` (the adjunction at every level), `P2_unit_is_iso`, `P2_counit_is_iso_forgettable`, and the summarizing `P2_grand_summary`.
[^6]

**This is *not* an equivalence.**  From the P1 obstruction it follows that `forget` is *not* total, and hence the adjunction is *not* an equivalence of categories (`complementarity_not_equivalence`): among the systems of level $LS L$ there are non-forgettable ones, which cannot be reached from below. This is the *asymmetry* of complementarity (`complementarity_asymmetry`): up-and-down returns the original ($`forget`\circ`embed`=\mathrm{id}$), but down-and-up covers only the forgettable part.

## Equivalence of Categories: Sameness up to Role

The adjunction of levels is a particular (and “tight”) case. The general notion “two categories are one and the same up to role” is *equivalence*. We build it as a ToS structure: `CatEquiv` $C D$ carries two functors $F:C\to D$, $G:D\to C$ and *natural isomorphisms* $\mathrm{id}_C\cong G\circ F$, $F\circ G\cong\mathrm{id}_D$. The inverses of the unit and the counit are carried as *data* (rather than as existence inside Prop), so the symmetry $D\simeq C$ is a mere permutation of fields (`equiv_sym`).
[^7] The unit and the counit turn out to be natural isomorphisms (`ce_unit_is_nat_iso`, `ce_counit_is_nat_iso`), and both functors are *essentially surjective* (`equiv_F_ess_surjective`, `equiv_G_ess_surjective`): every object of the target is isomorphic to the image of some object of the source. Reflexivity is given by the identity equivalence $C\simeq C$ (`id_equiv`), where all four natural transformations are identities.
[^8]

**An echo of P3.**  Equivalence is *weaker* than isomorphism of categories: what is required is only $G\circ F\cong\mathrm{id}$, not $G\circ F=\mathrm{id}$ “exactly.” This is a direct categorical echo of P3 from 14.3: it is natural to measure “sameness” *up to isomorphism*, not up to Leibniz equality.

**The contrast with levels.**  The adjunction $`embed`\dashv`forget`$ is even *tighter* than an equivalence requires (the unit is an isomorphism *always*, the counit an isomorphism *on the forgettable part*)—and yet it is *not* an equivalence: `embed` is not essentially surjective (the non-forgettable systems of P1). Hence $\Sys(L)$ sits in $\Sys(LS L)$ as a full role-subpart with a partial return (`forget`) on the forgettable objects, but does not exhaust it. Equivalence, for its part, is the right scale of “sameness up to role” for *whole* categories, and by it we will measure the dualities of the volume in 14.5.

## E/R/R Analysis: The Interlevel Structure as E/R/R

**Rules (L5).**  The functor laws of `embed` (preservation of identity/composition) + the laws of adjunction (triangles, naturality in both arguments) + the **P2 complementarity** ($`embed`\dashv`forget`$) + the **P1 obstruction** (`forget` is partial: $\neg L\ll L$). Equivalence sets the rule “sameness up to role” (an echo of P3).

**Roles (L4).**  `embed` is the left adjoint (a free-like embedding); `forget` is the right one (forgetful, partial); the unit is the role “the lower sits in the upper” (iso); the counit is the role “restore the forgettable”; equivalence is the role “the same up to role.”

{

**Elements (L1${}+{}$P4).**  Concrete data: `ElemOf`, transferred by `embed` one to one (`level_bridge`); the witness $w\ll L$, a computable “key” that opens forgetting; the system `witness_L_system` ($w=L$), a concrete *witness of the impossibility* of a total `forget`. *Not* an element of interlevel observability: the *totality* of `forget`—P1 rules it out directly (one cannot lower everything from $LS L$ to $L$).}

| **Component** | **What it determines** | **E/R/R category** |
|---|---|---|
| the functor laws of `embed` + triangles/naturality + P2 + the P1 obstruction | the constitution of the interlevel link | Rule (L5) |
| `embed`${}={}$ left adj.; `forget`${}={}$ right (partial); $\eta,\varepsilon$ as roles | the status of arrows between levels | Role (L4) |
| `ElemOf`; the witness $w\ll L$; `witness_L_system` | computable bearers and the “key” of forgetting | Element (L1${}+{}$P4) |

**P4 diagnostics.**  Why is `forget` partial? Because the hierarchy is *grounded*: there is no $L\ll L$, and a step down is not always possible—the level beneath must *really exist*. This is the same finite actuality that in 14.3 kept the empty/unit system from being placed at the bottom, not a defect: “lowering a level” makes sense only where there is somewhere to lower it to.

## What the Chapter Has Prepared

Functors between levels have been built: `embed`, a full-fledged faithful functor $\Sys(L)\to\Sys(LS L)$ that preserves embeddings/surjections/isomorphisms; `forget`, a partial forgetting limited by the P1 obstruction (`P1_obstructs_total_forget`, decidable). Their link is the adjunction $`embed`\dashv`forget`$ with a bijection of hom-sets, a unit that is an isomorphism (whence `embed` is fully faithful), and a counit that is an isomorphism on the forgettable systems; this is the machine form of the principle P2 (Complementarity). The adjunction is *not* an equivalence—precisely because of P1. The general frame “sameness up to role” is given by `CatEquiv` (symmetry, reflexivity, essential surjectivity), the categorical echo of P3.

**Tiers (honestly).**  The core of the adjunction—`LevelFunctors.v` (30 lemmas) + `LevelAdjunction.v` (25 Qed)—is **0 axioms, in `origin/main`**. The name P2 and the monad/comonad are in `ProcessAdjunction.v` (a synthetic layer that carries `classic`), also in `origin/main`. The equivalence frame—`EquivalenceOfCategories.v` (7 Qed) + `IdentityEquivalence.v` (2 Qed)—is 0 axioms but a **local layer** `src/category/` (commit `90ebd74`), awaiting `git push`.

Next comes Chapter 14.5: adjunctions $F\dashv G$ as a *universal language of dualities*; a right adjoint preserves limits (RAPL, the terminal case, by machine); and the *catalog of the volume’s adjunctions* (levels here / Galois from Part XI / geometry–gauge from Part XII) as a through-line.
